## Profile Title

AI SEO Content Systems for Ecommerce Brands

## Overview

I build AI-assisted SEO content systems for ecommerce and DTC brands that need product-aware content, not generic blog filler. In the iBolt case study, I compared 21 third-party SEO posts against pipeline-generated rewrites. The contractor posts averaged 1065 words, had 0 real product photos in the article body, and averaged 0 unique product URLs. The generated versions averaged 1286 words, included real product photos on 21 of 21 posts, and averaged 2 unique product URLs per post.

My work combines product catalog scraping, keyword and vertical research, brand-voice prompts, Shopify-ready HTML, and QA checks that look for missing product links, weak commercial intent, and unsupported claims. I can also build the internal tooling behind the content system, including React dashboards, Express APIs, SQLite/Postgres schemas, MCP surfaces, and automation workflows.

I am best for brands that already have products, customer questions, and messy content, but need a practical system to turn that material into useful search pages that can rank, educate, and send readers toward the right product.
