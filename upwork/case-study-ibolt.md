## Narrative

The contractor posts read like broad informational SEO pages: they averaged 1065 words, had no real product photos in the article body, and averaged 0 unique product links. The iBolt pipeline rewrote all 21 pages into buyer-problem guides with stronger titles, product-specific explanations, and real Shopify product photos. The generated versions averaged 1286 words, included real product photos on 21 of 21 posts, and averaged 2 unique product links per post. That matters because search visitors are not just looking for definitions. They need to know which mount fits their device, surface, vehicle, or workflow. Adding product evidence, compatibility language, and contextual internal links gives the page a clearer commercial path while keeping the copy educational enough to rank for informational and transactional searches.
