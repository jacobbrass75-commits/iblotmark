# ScholarMark SEO Handoff

Created: 2026-06-17

This folder is a handoff packet for publishing ScholarMark SEO, FAQ, and landing-page support content.

The goal is to position ScholarMark as a source-grounded academic writing workspace for serious students, without implying that it writes assignments for them or guarantees perfect citations.

## Core Positioning

Find the quote. Keep the context. Write from evidence.

ScholarMark helps students build a searchable source base, keep quote context attached, create citation notes, outline from selected evidence, draft student-owned writing, and verify quotes, citations, and claims before export.

## Important Guardrail

Do not say "guaranteed no hallucinations" or "always accurate citations." The safer and stronger claim is:

ScholarMark helps students reduce the risk of unsupported or hallucinated quotes by drafting from passages they can inspect, cite, and verify.
