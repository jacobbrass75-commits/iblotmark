# Positioning And Guardrails

## One-Line Positioning

ScholarMark is a source-grounded academic writing workspace for students who need to find quotes, keep context, organize sources, and verify evidence before submitting serious research work.

## Core Promise

Find the quote. Keep the context. Write from evidence.

## Longer Positioning

ScholarMark helps students search a large source base for useful passages, keep surrounding context attached, build citation notes, create evidence-backed outlines, and draft student-owned writing they can verify. It should be framed as planning, source organization, quote/context support, citation-note support, outlining, argument feedback, and revision support.

## Audience

- Rising seniors starting a thesis, capstone, honors paper, senior seminar, or major research paper.
- Rising juniors preparing for research methods, upper-level seminars, honors work, grad/law school writing, or research-heavy classes.
- Students in writing-heavy majors: history, English, political science, international relations, sociology, psychology, philosophy, anthropology, education, environmental studies, religious studies, gender studies, ethnic studies, public policy, and communications.
- Campus channel owners: honors offices, undergraduate research offices, writing centers, department advisors, student org leaders, professional fraternities, Greek academic chairs, and scholarship chairs.

## Approved Claims

- Source-grounded writing support.
- Draft from selected evidence.
- Keep citations connected to quotes and source context.
- Build a searchable source base before drafting.
- Find passages across uploaded PDFs, scans, pasted text, and web clips.
- Review quotes, citations, paraphrases, and unsupported claims before export.
- Designed to reduce the risk of unsupported or hallucinated quotes.
- Helps students document their research and writing process.
- Works as support for student-owned work.

## Banned Claims

- "Guaranteed no hallucinations."
- "Always accurate citations."
- "Writes your paper for you."
- "Get an A automatically."
- "Beat Turnitin."
- "Undetectable AI writing."
- "Plagiarism-free by default."
- "No need to verify sources."
- "Replaces your professor, librarian, writing center, or Zotero."

## Tone

Clear, student-aware, premium but not stiff. Sound like a smart writing coach and research partner, not a hypey AI startup.

Use:

- "source-grounded writing"
- "evidence review"
- "citation-connected drafts"
- "quote context"
- "verification before export"
- "student-owned work"

Avoid:

- revolutionary
- game-changing
- unlock your potential
- humanize to beat AI detection
- AI-powered everything
- imagine a world
- hallucination-free
