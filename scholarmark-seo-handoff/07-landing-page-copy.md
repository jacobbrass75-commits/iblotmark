## Section: Why Blank Chat Feels Risky For Academic Writing

Generic AI can produce confident writing before the sources are settled. That is the problem. A polished paragraph is not enough when your professor, advisor, or writing center expects you to explain where the claim came from and why the citation supports it.

ScholarMark starts earlier in the process. Before you draft, you build the source base. Before you quote, you save the surrounding context. Before you submit, you check the evidence.

## Section: Source Base First, Writing Second

Use ScholarMark to collect the materials that actually belong in your paper:

- PDFs and scans.
- Pasted text and notes.
- Web clips with URL and context.
- Annotations and useful passages.
- Citation notes and source metadata.

Then use that source base to plan, outline, and revise with AI support that stays connected to the evidence you selected.

## Section: Quote Context Matters

A quote can be real and still be used badly. ScholarMark helps keep the surrounding passage visible so you can check what the author meant, what came before, what came after, and whether the quote really supports your claim.

That matters for theses, capstones, honors papers, senior seminars, research-heavy classes, and any paper where your argument depends on careful evidence.

## Section: Built For Student-Owned Work

ScholarMark is not a shortcut for turning in AI-generated assignments. It is a workspace for planning, evidence review, outlining, revision, citation notes, and verification.

Use it to make your own thinking easier to defend.
