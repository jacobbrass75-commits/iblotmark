# Multi-Agent Research Summary

The research session used four lanes:

1. SEO keyword and search-intent research.
2. Consumer/student language and pain points.
3. Competitor positioning scan.
4. Local ScholarMark architecture and campaign mapping.

## Lane 1: SEO Keyword Findings

Prioritized clusters:

- AI writing with sources
- hallucinated quotes AI
- source grounded AI writing
- citation verification AI
- quote verification
- organize sources research paper
- best AI writing tool for students
- RAG writing tool for students
- AI academic integrity writing
- capstone writing tool

Key conclusion:

Use "best AI writing tool for students" and "best AI writing app" as comparison intent, but do not promise no hallucinations. Reframe as "best for source-grounded writing and quote verification."

## Lane 2: Consumer Language Findings

Repeated language:

- AI makes up quotes.
- Citations look real but cannot be found.
- Students want AI to answer from uploaded sources.
- Source overload creates executive-function pressure.
- Students want support, not a generate-and-submit shortcut.
- Academic integrity anxiety is about process and proof.

Key conclusion:

The most engaging content should open with a real student moment:

"You found the perfect quote, then could not find it in the PDF."

## Lane 3: Competitor Findings

Competitors:

- Grammarly
- Jenni AI
- Paperpal
- QuillBot
- Elicit
- SciSpace
- Consensus
- NotebookLM
- Perplexity
- Zotero
- Scite

Key conclusion:

ScholarMark should own the intersection of student writing workflow, source organization, quote context, citation notes, and verification. NotebookLM is the strongest comparison threat, so comparison pages should be precise and fair.

## Lane 4: Local Product Mapping

Local architecture supports these messages:

- source base
- project search
- annotations
- citation notes
- writing pipeline
- chat compile and verify
- quote jump links
- web clips
- OCR/source ingest

Key conclusion:

Use product proof rather than vague AI claims. Show sources, quote queue, citation rail, draft, and verification checklist.

## Final Recommended SEO Spine

Hub page:

- AI Writing With Real Sources

Trust pages:

- How To Avoid Getting Burned By Hallucinated Quotes
- Citation Verification AI
- AI And Academic Integrity

How-to pages:

- How To Organize Sources For A Long Research Paper
- How To Build A Quote Bank
- Thesis Outline From Sources

Comparison pages:

- ScholarMark vs NotebookLM
- ScholarMark vs Grammarly Citation Finder
- ScholarMark vs Jenni AI
- ScholarMark vs Zotero
