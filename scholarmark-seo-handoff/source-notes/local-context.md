## Product Summary From Architecture

ScholarMark is a research and writing workspace. Users upload or paste sources, run AI-assisted annotation and citation workflows, organize evidence into projects, clip evidence from the web, and draft or verify writing through chat and writing tools.

## Feature-To-Message Map

| Product feature | Safe public message | Content angle |
|---|---|---|
| Projects and folders | Build a searchable source base before drafting. | Thesis source base; organize sources for research paper |
| Project search | Search project summaries, document context, and annotation content. | Find the passage before you quote it |
| AI/manual annotations | Save useful passages and keep context attached. | Quote bank; source-grounded writing |
| Citation endpoints | Create citation notes, footnotes, bibliography entries, and quote-attached footnotes you can verify. | AI citations research papers; citation verification |
| Chat compile and verify | Draft and review writing against selected source context. | Write from evidence; verify before export |
| Writing pipeline | Turn evidence into plan, sections, and final markdown. | Thesis outline from sources; evidence-to-paragraph workflow |
| Quote jump links | Jump from compiled markdown back to source passages. | Avoid hallucinated quotes; quote verification |
| Web clips | Save URL, author/date/category, note, and surrounding context. | Source organization; research process record |
| OCR/document ingest | Bring PDFs, scans, images, and pasted text into one workspace. | Large source base; Summer Thesis Head Start |

## Design Positioning From Local DESIGN.md

Approved framing:

- ScholarMark is a research and writing workspace for source-grounded academic work.
- Research writing without losing the evidence.
- A workspace for source-grounded papers.
- Write from your sources, not from scratch.
- Draft from selected evidence.
- Verify claims before export.

Avoid:

- generic chatbot framing
- homework shortcut framing
- detector-evasion framing
- fake proof or fake institutional claims
- hype words and vague AI promises
