## What is ScholarMark?

ScholarMark is a source-grounded research writing workspace for students. It helps you collect sources, find useful passages, keep quote context attached, organize citation notes, build outlines, and review drafts against your evidence.

## Who is ScholarMark for?

ScholarMark is for students working on research-heavy writing: theses, capstones, honors papers, senior seminars, literature reviews, long class papers, and writing samples. It is especially useful when you have many PDFs, web sources, notes, quotes, and citations to keep straight.

## Does ScholarMark write my paper for me?

No. ScholarMark is designed to support student-owned work. It helps you organize evidence, plan sections, draft from selected sources, and verify quotes and citations, but the argument, judgment, and final submission remain yours.

## How does ScholarMark help with quotes?

ScholarMark helps you search your source base for useful passages and keep context attached. That makes it easier to check whether a quote is real, where it appears, and whether the surrounding context supports your use of it.

## Can ScholarMark help with citations?

Yes. ScholarMark can support citation notes, footnotes, bibliography entries, and quote-connected citation workflows. You should still verify final citation details and required style before submitting.

## Is ScholarMark allowed under academic integrity policies?

That depends on your assignment and institution. ScholarMark should be used as support for planning, organizing sources, reviewing evidence, and revising student-owned work. Always check your professor's policy before using AI-assisted tools for graded work.

## What is Summer Thesis Head Start?

Summer Thesis Head Start is a practical early-start workflow for students who want to make progress on a thesis, capstone, honors paper, senior seminar, research project, or long paper before fall gets busy.

## Can I use ScholarMark for a capstone?

Yes. Capstone projects often involve sources, notes, background research, evidence, and a final written deliverable. ScholarMark can help you build a source base, organize quote context, create an outline, and review the draft before submission.

## Can I use ScholarMark for an honors thesis?

Yes. ScholarMark is a good fit for honors thesis planning because it supports the early stages students often delay: source collection, research-question development, quote organization, outline building, and evidence review.

## What should I verify before submitting?

Verify the final quote text, source location, citation metadata, bibliography format, paraphrase accuracy, and your instructor or school policy on AI-assisted work.
