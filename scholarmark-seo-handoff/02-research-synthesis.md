# Research Synthesis

## Executive Takeaway

The best ScholarMark SEO lane is not "AI writer that never hallucinates." That claim is risky and not credible. The stronger lane is:

Source-grounded academic writing for serious students who need quote context, citation notes, source organization, and verification before export.

Students are already using AI, but trust is the gap. HEPI's 2025 student survey reported that 92% of students used AI tools, while 51% cited false results or hallucinations as a reason they avoid AI. A Scientific Reports study found fabricated citation rates in tested ChatGPT outputs, including 55% for GPT-3.5 citations and 18% for GPT-4 citations. These sources support the need for a verification-first workflow.

Sources:

- [HEPI Student Generative AI Survey 2025](https://www.hepi.ac.uk/reports/student-generative-ai-survey-2025/)
- [Scientific Reports: Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-41032-5)

## Consumer Language Themes

Students and instructors describe five repeated pain points:

1. AI citations look real, then fall apart when checked.
2. Direct quotes are harder than citations because the wording must match exactly.
3. Source organization breaks down as projects grow past 20, 50, or 100 sources.
4. Students want AI to work only from material they upload or verify.
5. Academic integrity anxiety is about process, not just detection.

Good language to reuse:

- "wasting more time chasing down and verifying every claim"
- "use AI for outlining and fill in the facts yourself"
- "AI works only within that vetted corpus"
- "getting lost"
- "generate to submit is not the workflow"
- "source base first, writing second"

See `source-notes/consumer-language.md` for direct quotes and URLs.

## Competitor Pattern

Most competitors cluster around one of four jobs:

- Broad writing polish: Grammarly, QuillBot.
- Academic writing assistance: Jenni, Paperpal, SciSpace.
- Research discovery and literature synthesis: Elicit, Consensus, Scite.
- Source-grounded notebook/chat: NotebookLM.

ScholarMark should not claim to beat all of them at their core job. It should own the intersection:

Student writing workflow + uploaded source base + annotations + quote context + citation notes + draft verification.

## ScholarMark-Owned Differentiators

1. Quote verification, not just citation formatting.
2. Source annotations and searchable project evidence.
3. Citation-connected drafting, not blank-chat drafting.
4. Quote jump links from draft back to source passages.
5. Academic integrity posture that documents process instead of promising detector evasion.
6. Summer Thesis Head Start as a practical campaign wrapper.

## Best SEO Angle

Use bottom-funnel language that still respects trust:

- Best AI writing app for source-grounded academic work.
- AI writing with real sources.
- How to avoid hallucinated quotes.
- Citation and quote verification workflow.
- RAG writing tool for students.
- Source organizer for thesis and capstone writing.

Do not use "best AI writing app for no hallucinations" as a promise. It can be used as an intent target in metadata and FAQ language, but the page must correct the premise:

No AI writing tool can honestly guarantee zero hallucinations. The safer question is whether the tool helps you write from source passages you can inspect and verify.
