# Competitor Comparison Page Strategy

Comparison pages should be factual, restrained, and non-attacky. Use them to clarify jobs-to-be-done.

## Comparison Page Rules

- Do not claim competitors are unsafe or dishonest.
- Use official competitor claims only where possible.
- Avoid invented benchmarks or fake screenshots.
- Keep the frame: "which tool fits which workflow?"
- Include an academic integrity note on every comparison page.
- Link to source-grounded core pages and Summer Thesis CTA.

## Highest-Priority Comparison Pages

Target keyword: ScholarMark vs NotebookLM, NotebookLM for research papers, NotebookLM thesis writing

Position:

NotebookLM is strong for source-grounded chat and source summaries. ScholarMark should be positioned as a writing workspace for academic projects where quote context, citation notes, annotations, source organization, draft review, and export readiness matter.

## Comparison FAQ Snippets

### Is ScholarMark a replacement for Zotero?

Not necessarily. Zotero is excellent for collecting, organizing, and citing references. ScholarMark is built for students who want to search their source base, keep quote context attached, draft from selected evidence, and verify claims before export.

### Is NotebookLM enough for a thesis?

NotebookLM can be useful for source-grounded questions and summaries. For thesis writing, many students also need project organization, quote banks, citation notes, outline support, draft review, and a verification workflow.

### Is Grammarly enough for citations?

Grammarly can help with citation suggestions and formatting. ScholarMark focuses on the deeper research workflow: where the quote came from, what context surrounds it, and whether the claim is supported by selected evidence.
