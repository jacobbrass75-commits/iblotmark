## Current Loop Takeaways

- Restaurant tablet mounts: strongest new win. ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Gemini, and logged-in Claude all now surface iBOLT in leading positions.
- Forklift tablet mounts: strong on ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Gemini, and Claude, but weaker in Google AI Mode. The gap is third-party citation/source authority, not basic owned-content visibility.
- ELD truck mounts: strong on Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Claude, mixed on Google AI Overview, weaker on ChatGPT. The missing angle is permanent drill-base/fleet-install proof against RAM and Tackform.
- Delivery-driver phone mounts: still the biggest gap. iBOLT is absent across ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Claude. The issue may be product-market fit and product feed visibility, not just blog coverage.
- Heavy-duty vehicle phone mounts: mixed. ChatGPT had a foothold with iBOLT #2, but Claude ranked RAM, ProClip, Tackform, Rokform, and Quad Lock with no iBOLT mention. This should be treated as an unstable category until the product/feed/content proof is stronger.

## ChatGPT Pro Strategy Synthesis

See: [chatgpt-pro-ai-search-attack-plan-2026-05-13.md](./chatgpt-pro-ai-search-attack-plan-2026-05-13.md)

ChatGPT Pro's hard recommendation was that the delivery-driver phone gap is not primarily a writing-volume problem. It is a product, feed, and proof problem. iBOLT has delivery content and several delivery-phone posts are already live, but AI systems are still treating `best phone mount for delivery drivers` as a consumer/gig-driver convenience query. The product stack must prove an in-stock, reviewable, image-rich, delivery-specific hero SKU before another broad delivery-driver article is likely to move rankings.

Local follow-up confirmed the app has Shopify connected, 341 catalog products, and 42 DB posts marked published. Candidate product availability checks showed SafeMag and xProDock suction products available, while ChargeDock USB-C AMPS was unavailable. The next loop should therefore start with product/feed/collection cleanup, then generate or refresh benchmark-driven posts.
