## Raw Answer

For a restaurant POS and delivery app tablet mount, both RAM Mounts and iBOLT offer robust solutions, but they cater to slightly different priorities. Based on your needs for commercial use, durability, and specific applications, here's a breakdown and ranking:

## Ranking the Strongest Options:

### 1. RAM Mounts (Overall Best for Durability, Modularity, and Commercial Use)

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the industry standard for heavy-duty, customizable mounting solutions. Their "ball and socket" system is incredibly versatile and durable, making them ideal for the demanding environment of a restaurant.

**Commercial Use Cases:**

*   **Fixed POS Stations:** Mount tablets securely to countertops, walls, or even the side of a kitchen printer.
*   **Mobile POS (Server Handhelds):** While not their primary strength for *handheld* use, RAM offers solutions for mounting tablets to carts or even belt clips for larger tablets.
*   **Kitchen Display Systems (KDS):** Mount tablets in harsh kitchen environments, knowing they can withstand splashes and vibrations.
*   **Delivery App Stations:** Securely mount tablets for order management, driver tracking, and dispatch.
*   **Customer-Facing Displays:** Mount tablets for digital menus, loyalty programs, or self-ordering kiosks.

**Durability:**

*   **Unmatched:** RAM Mounts are built to last. Components are typically made from high-strength composite, marine-grade aluminum, or stainless steel. They are designed to withstand constant adjustments, vibrations, and the occasional bump or spill.
*   **Vibration Dampening:** The rubber ball and socket design inherently provides some vibration dampening, crucial in a busy kitchen or near noisy equipment.
*   **Corrosion Resistance:** Many components are designed for marine environments, making them resistant to rust and corrosion from cleaning agents or food spills.

**Installation Style:**

*   **Highly Modular:** This is RAM's biggest strength. You choose individual components:
    *   **Base:** Suction cup, C-clamp, drill-down (screws), track mount, U-bolt, adhesive.
    *   **Arm:** Various lengths (short, medium, long) and materials.
    *   **Holder:** Universal X-Grip, Tab-Tite (spring-loaded), Tab-Lock (key-locking for security), or custom-fit cradles for specific tablet models.
*   **Secure:** Drill-down bases offer the most permanent and secure installation, essential for high-traffic POS. C-clamps are great for temporary or adjustable setups.
*   **Tool-Free Adjustment (mostly):** Once installed, the ball and socket system allows for easy, tool-free adjustment of viewing angles.

**Tradeoffs:**

*   **Cost:** Generally more expensive than iBOLT, especially when building a complete system with multiple components.
*   **Bulkier Appearance:** The industrial design, while functional, might not be as sleek as some iBOLT options.
*   **Initial Complexity:** The sheer number of options can be overwhelming for first-time buyers. You need to understand which base, arm, and holder combination is right for your specific tablet and mounting location.

**Recommended RAM Mount Components for Restaurant Use:**

*   **Holders:**
    *   **RAM Tab-Lock™ or Tab-Tite™:** For dedicated POS tablets, these offer a secure, spring-loaded or key-locking grip.
    *   **RAM X-Grip®:** Excellent for tablets that might be swapped out frequently or for different sizes. Less secure than Tab-Lock but very versatile.
*   **Arms:**
    *   **RAM Double Socket Arm (Medium):** A good all-around choice for flexibility.
*   **Bases:**
    *   **RAM 2.5" Round Base with AMPs Hole Pattern (RAM-B-202U):** For drill-down installations.
    *   **RAM C-Clamp Mount (RAM-B-201U-C):** For mounting to tables, counters, or poles without drilling.
    *   **RAM Tough-Claw™ (RAM-B-400U):** For quick, tool-less clamping to various surfaces.

### 2. iBOLT (Strong Contender for Specific Use Cases, Value, and Sleeker Design)

**Why it's #2:** iBOLT offers excellent value and often a more integrated, sleeker design, making them a strong choice for certain restaurant applications, especially where aesthetics or a slightly lower price point are priorities.

**Commercial Use Cases:**

*   **Dedicated POS Stations:** Many iBOLT mounts are designed specifically for POS, often with integrated charging.
*   **Delivery App Stations:** Their robust holders are great for securing tablets used for order management.
*   **Customer-Facing Kiosks:** Some iBOLT mounts have a cleaner look that might be preferred for customer-facing applications.
*   **Vehicle Mounts (for delivery drivers):** iBOLT has a strong line of vehicle mounts, which could be relevant if your restaurant has its own delivery fleet.
