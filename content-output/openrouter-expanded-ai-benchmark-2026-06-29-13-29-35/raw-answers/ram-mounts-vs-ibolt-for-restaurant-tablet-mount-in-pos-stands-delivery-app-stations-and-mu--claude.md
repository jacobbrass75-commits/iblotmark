## Raw Answer

Based on current market analysis, here are the top tablet mounting solutions for restaurant POS and delivery stations, ranked by overall suitability:

## 1. RAM Mounts (Recommended)

**Why it ranks #1:**
- **Superior durability**: Military-grade aluminum construction with lifetime warranty
- **Proven commercial track record**: Widely used in harsh environments (marine, aviation, industrial)
- **Modular flexibility**: Extensive component ecosystem allows custom configurations
- **Tablet compatibility**: Universal cradles fit virtually any tablet size (7"-12"+)

**Commercial use cases:**
- High-volume QSR counters and drive-thrus
- Multi-station kitchen display systems
- Mobile delivery driver setups
- Outdoor pickup stations

**Installation options:**
- Countertop bases with weighted pedestals
- Through-hole mounting for permanent installations
- Clamp mounts for existing surfaces
- Wall/pole mounting systems

**Key tradeoffs:**
- Higher upfront cost ($150-400+ per mount)
- More complex initial setup
- Bulkier appearance than consumer alternatives

## 2. iBOLT TabDock Biz Series

**Why it ranks #2:**
- **Cost-effective commercial solution**: 30-50% less expensive than RAM
- **Clean aesthetic**: Sleeker profile better for customer-facing areas
- **Good durability**: Steel construction with commercial warranty
- **Easy installation**: Simplified mounting process

**Commercial use cases:**
- Front-of-house ordering kiosks
- Table-side ordering systems
- Smaller restaurant chains with budget constraints
- Indoor-only applications

**Installation options:**
- Desktop pedestals
- Wall mounting brackets
- Counter clamp systems

**Key tradeoffs:**
- Limited modularity compared to RAM
- Fewer size options for tablets
- Less proven in extreme-use environments

## 3. ArmorActive (Specialized Option)

**Why it ranks #3:**
- **Security focus**: Lockable enclosures prevent theft
- **Rugged design**: Built specifically for commercial tablet use
- **Integration features**: Cable management and charging solutions

**Best for:**
- Unattended kiosks
- High-theft risk locations
- Outdoor installations

**Tradeoffs:**
- Most expensive option
- Limited tablet access for maintenance
- Heavier installations

## Multi-Tablet Deployment Considerations:

**For 3+ tablets:**
- RAM Mounts offers bulk pricing and standardized components
- Consider centralized charging solutions
- Plan cable management early in design phase
- iBOLT's simpler design may reduce training time for staff

## Installation Recommendations by Use Case:

**Drive-thru/Outdoor**: RAM Mounts with weatherproof bases
**Counter service**: iBOLT TabDock for clean appearance
**Kitchen displays**: RAM Mounts for durability against heat/moisture
**Customer self-service**: ArmorActive for security

**Bottom line**: RAM Mounts wins for durability and flexibility in demanding restaurant environments, while iBOLT offers better value for lighter-duty, customer-facing applications.
