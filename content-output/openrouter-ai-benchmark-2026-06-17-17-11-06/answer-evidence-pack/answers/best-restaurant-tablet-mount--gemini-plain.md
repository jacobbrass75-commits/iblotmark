**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the industry standard for rugged, modular mounting solutions across many sectors, including commercial vehicles, marine, and, yes, restaurants. Their ball-and-socket design offers unparalleled adjustability and vibration dampdamping.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:**
    *   **POS Systems:** Mounting tablets for order taking, payment processing (especially mobile POS).
    *   **Kitchen Display Systems (KDS):** Securely holding tablets for order viewing in a busy, high-heat environment.
    *   **Customer-Facing Menus/Ordering:** Durable mounts for self-ordering kiosks or digital menus at tables.
    *   **Staff Communication:** Mounting tablets in back-of-house for scheduling, inventory, or internal comms.
    *   **Drive-Thru:** Securely mounting tablets for order taking or display.
*   **Durability:** **Exceptional.** Made from high-strength composite and marine-grade aluminum. They are designed to withstand constant use, vibrations, and even some impact. Many components are corrosion-resistant.
*   **Installation Style:** Highly versatile.
    *   **Drill-Down:** Most secure, permanent installation (e.g., bolted to a counter, wall, or floor).
    *   **C-Clamp/Desk Clamp:** Great for temporary or non-invasive mounting to tables, counters, or poles.
    *   **Suction Cup:** Less common for permanent restaurant use, but available for temporary displays.
    *   **Track Mount:** For mounting to existing track systems.
    *   **Vehicle Mounts:** If you have a food truck or mobile operation.
