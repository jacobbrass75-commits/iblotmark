# Commercial Grade Phone Mounts for Delivery Drivers

## 4. **Mob Armor Magnetic Mount System**
**Why it's #4:** Fastest phone changes for multi-stop routes
- **Durability:** Neodymium magnets with rubberized coating

## Key Commercial Considerations:
- **Fleet standardization:** RAM and ProClip offer bulk pricing
- **Insurance compliance:** Ensure mount doesn't obstruct driver vision
- **Phone compatibility:** Consider various driver phone sizes (iPhone 12-15, Samsung Galaxy series)
- **Temperature resistance:** All recommended options handle -20°F to 140°F operating ranges

**Bottom line:** For serious commercial use, invest in RAM Mounts for maximum reliability, or ProClip for dedicated vehicles with professional appearance requirements.
