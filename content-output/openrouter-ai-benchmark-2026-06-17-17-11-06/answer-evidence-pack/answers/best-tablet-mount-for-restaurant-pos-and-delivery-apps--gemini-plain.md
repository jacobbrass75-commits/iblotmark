## Top Tablet Mounts for Restaurant POS and Delivery Apps

### 1. RAM Mounts (Various Series - X-Grip, Tab-Tite, Tab-Lock)

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the undisputed champions of industrial-grade tablet mounting. Their modular system allows for incredible customization, and their components are built to last in demanding environments.

**Commercial Use Cases:**
*   **Fixed POS Stations:** Securely mount tablets at cashier stations, order entry points, or kitchen display systems (KDS).
*   **Mobile POS (with a cart):** While the mount itself isn't mobile, it can be attached to a mobile cart for tableside ordering or payment.
*   **Delivery App Hubs:** Create dedicated stations for managing multiple delivery tablets.
*   **Customer-Facing Kiosks:** Durable enough for self-ordering or loyalty program sign-ups.

**Durability:**
*   **Unmatched:** Made from high-strength composite, marine-grade aluminum, and stainless steel. They are designed to withstand vibration, impact, and constant use.
*   **Weather/Spill Resistant:** Many components are resistant to spills and can be easily wiped down.
*   **Long Lifespan:** These mounts are an investment that will likely outlast the tablets they hold.

**Installation Style:**
*   **Highly Versatile:**
    *   **Drill-Down:** Most secure option, bolting directly to countertops, walls, or tables.
    *   **C-Clamp:** Excellent for temporary or non-permanent installations on tables, counters, or shelves.
    *   **Suction Cup (Heavy Duty):** For smooth, non-porous surfaces, though less recommended for high-traffic POS.
    *   **Track Mount:** Integrates with existing track systems.
    *   **Adhesive Base:** For less invasive, but still strong, surface mounting.
*   **Modular System:** Consists of a base, an arm (various lengths and articulation points), and a tablet holder. This allows you to build the exact mount you need.

**Tradeoffs:**
*   **Cost:** Generally more expensive than other options, but the durability justifies the price.
*   **Aesthetics:** While functional, the industrial look might not fit all restaurant aesthetics.
*   **Complexity:** The modularity can be overwhelming initially, requiring some research to select the right components.
*   **Security (Tab-Lock Series):** While the X-Grip and Tab-Tite are secure, the Tab-Lock series adds a key-locking mechanism for theft prevention, which is crucial in public-facing POS.

**Recommended RAM Mount Components:**
*   **Tablet Holder:**
    *   **RAM X-Grip:** Spring-loaded, universal fit for many tablet sizes (e.g., RAM-HOL-UN9U for 9-10" tablets). Great for quick insertion/removal.
    *   **RAM Tab-Tite:** Form-fitting, adjustable cradle for specific tablet models or ranges. More secure than X-Grip.
    *   **RAM Tab-Lock:** Key-locking version of the Tab-Tite, offering theft deterrence. **Highly recommended for public-facing or unattended POS.**
*   **Arm:** Standard double socket arms (e.g., RAM-B-201U for medium length) are a good starting point.
*   **Base:** RAM-B-202U (round base with AMPS hole pattern for drill-down), RAM-B-202U-225 (C-clamp), or RAM-B-238U (diamond base for various attachments).
