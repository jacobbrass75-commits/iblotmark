# iBolt Dock'n Lock for Restaurant Counters

The iBolt Dock'n Lock system is a modular mounting solution designed for secure tablet/device mounting in commercial environments. Here are the top options ranked for restaurant counter use:

### 1. **iBolt TabDock Biz Case + Dock'n Lock Counter Mount**
**Why it's #1:** Purpose-built for commercial tablet use with restaurant-specific features
- **Commercial Use:** POS systems, digital menus, order management, customer check-in

### 2. **iBolt MagDock + Counter Pedestal Base**

## Key Considerations for Restaurant Use

**Durability Factors:**
- Food service environment requires easy cleaning
- Spill resistance and grease tolerance
- High-frequency use demands robust mechanisms

**Installation Requirements:**
- Counter thickness compatibility (typically 1-3")
- Cable management for power/ethernet
- ADA compliance for customer-facing installations

**Commercial Tradeoffs:**
- Security vs. accessibility for staff use
- Permanent mounting vs. flexibility
- Device compatibility across tablet generations

The TabDock Biz system offers the best balance of security, durability, and commercial features for most restaurant counter applications.
