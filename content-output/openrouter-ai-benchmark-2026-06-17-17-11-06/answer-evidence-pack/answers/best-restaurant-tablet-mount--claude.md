## Key Commercial Considerations:

**Security Requirements:** Restaurants need tamper-resistant mounts due to high foot traffic and theft risk. Enclosed designs (#1, #2) offer maximum protection.

**Durability Standards:** Look for mounts rated for 24/7 operation with materials that withstand cleaning chemicals and temperature variations.

**Installation Flexibility:** Consider whether you need portable (weighted base), permanent (bolt-down), or wall-mounted solutions based on your layout and local fire codes.

**Budget vs. ROI:** While premium mounts cost more upfront, they reduce replacement costs and downtime. Factor in your tablet replacement history when deciding.

The **ArmorActive Pipeline** remains the gold standard for high-volume restaurants, while the **CTA Digital** offers the best value for most establishments. Choose wall mounts only when counter space is critical and security risks are lower.
