# Best Fish Finder Mounts for Small Boats

Here are the top fish finder mount options for small boats, ranked by versatility and performance:

## 1. **RAM Mounts RAM-109H-TAB3U (Adjustable Arm Mount)**
**Why it's #1:** Most versatile and durable option
- **Commercial use:** Excellent for charter/guide boats - withstands daily use
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum, corrosion-resistant
- **Installation:** Clamps to gunwale, console, or rail (no drilling required)
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($80-120), but worth it for adjustability and build quality

## 2. **Scotty #135 Transducer Arm Mount**
**Why it's here:** Best for portable setups and kayaks/small skiffs
- **Commercial use:** Good for rental fleets - easy setup/removal
- **Durability:** Reinforced nylon, UV-resistant
- **Installation:** Clamp-on design, works with most small boat rails
- **Tradeoffs:** Less rigid than RAM mounts, but more affordable ($40-60)

## 3. **Humminbird MS M Universal Mount**
**Why it's ranked:** Brand-specific optimization with good stability
- **Commercial use:** Solid for dedicated Humminbird setups
- **Durability:** Composite construction, adequate for recreational use
- **Installation:** Flush mount or bracket mount options
- **Tradeoffs:** Limited to Humminbird units, less universal ($30-50)

## 4. **Lowrance/Simrad Gimbal Bracket**
**Why it's here:** Excellent shock absorption for rough water
- **Commercial use:** Great for offshore small boats (center consoles)
- **Durability:** Stainless steel hardware, built for marine environment
- **Installation:** Requires drilling/permanent mounting
- **Tradeoffs:** Unit-specific, permanent installation required ($25-40)

## Key Considerations for Small Boats:
- **Vibration dampening** is crucial - small boats transmit more engine/wave vibration
- **Quick-release capability** helps prevent theft and allows easy seasonal removal
- **Adjustable viewing angles** are essential since small boat seating positions vary
- **Compact footprint** to avoid interfering with limited deck space

**Best overall recommendation:** RAM Mount system for durability and flexibility, unless you need brand-specific features or have budget constraints.
