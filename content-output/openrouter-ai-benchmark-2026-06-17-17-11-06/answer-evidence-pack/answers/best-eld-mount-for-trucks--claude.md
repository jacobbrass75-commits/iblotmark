# Best ELD Mounts for Commercial Trucks

Here are the top ELD mounting solutions ranked by reliability, durability, and commercial suitability:

## 1. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Series**
**Why it's #1:** Industry standard for heavy-duty commercial applications
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, vibration-dampening ball joints
- **Installation:** Suction cup, permanent drill-mount, or clamp options
- **Commercial use:** Handles constant vibration, extreme temperatures (-20°F to 180°F)
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($40-80), but virtually indestructible

## 2. **Arkon Heavy-Duty Truck Mount**
**Why it's ranked here:** Purpose-built for trucking with excellent stability
- **Durability:** Steel construction with reinforced joints
- **Installation:** Clamps to air vents or mounts to dashboard/windshield
- **Commercial use:** Designed for 8+ hour daily use, fits tablets 7"-12"
- **Tradeoffs:** Bulkier profile, limited adjustability vs RAM

## 3. **ProClip USA Custom Dash Mounts**
**Why it's #3:** Vehicle-specific fit eliminates movement
- **Durability:** ABS plastic, less robust than metal alternatives
- **Installation:** Custom clips designed for specific truck models (no drilling)
- **Commercial use:** Clean professional appearance, doesn't obstruct controls
- **Tradeoffs:** Must buy truck-specific mount, limited to lighter tablets

## 4. **Mob Armor Magnetic Mounts**
**Why it's #4:** Quick deployment but limited heavy-duty applications
- **Durability:** Strong neodymium magnets, but relies on device case
- **Installation:** Magnetic base adheres to metal surfaces
- **Commercial use:** Easy device removal for inspections
- **Tradeoffs:** Not suitable for heavy vibration routes, requires magnetic case

## Key Commercial Considerations:

**DOT Compliance:** Ensure mount doesn't obstruct driver view (typically mount right of steering wheel)

**Vibration Resistance:** Long-haul trucks need mounts rated for constant road vibration

**Security:** Consider locking mounts for high-theft areas

**Cable Management:** Look for mounts with integrated charging cable routing

**Device Compatibility:** Verify mount works with your specific ELD tablet/device dimensions

The **RAM X-Grip** consistently ranks highest among fleet managers due to its proven durability in demanding commercial environments, despite the higher upfront cost.
