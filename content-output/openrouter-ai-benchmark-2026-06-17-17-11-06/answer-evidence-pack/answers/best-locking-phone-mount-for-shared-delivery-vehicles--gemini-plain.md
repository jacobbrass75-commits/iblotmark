## Raw Answer

For shared delivery vehicles, a "best locking phone mount" needs to prioritize **security, durability, ease of use for multiple drivers, and adaptability to various vehicle interiors.** Given the high-turnover nature of shared vehicles and the potential for theft or damage, a truly robust solution is essential.

Here's a breakdown of the best options, ranked from strongest to weakest, with detailed explanations:

---

### **Top Recommendation: RAM Mounts with Locking Knob/Keyed Lock**

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the industry standard for heavy-duty, modular mounting solutions. Their robust construction, vast customization options, and specific locking mechanisms make them ideal for shared delivery vehicles.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:**
    *   **Delivery Fleets (UPS, FedEx, Amazon Flex, DoorDash, Uber Eats):** Perfect for ensuring drivers have a secure, visible navigation device that won't fall or be easily stolen.
    *   **Field Service Technicians:** For vehicles used by multiple technicians who need consistent, reliable access to their work orders on a tablet or phone.
    *   **Rental Car Fleets (with modifications):** While not typically installed by rental companies, a secure mount could be a value-add for long-term rentals or specialized services.
*   **Durability:**
    *   **Military-Grade:** Often used in military, marine, and aviation applications. Constructed from marine-grade aluminum, high-strength composites, and stainless steel.
    *   **Vibration Dampening:** The ball-and-socket design inherently absorbs vibrations, protecting the phone and maintaining stability on rough roads.
    *   **Weather Resistant:** Most components are designed to withstand extreme temperatures and moisture.
*   **Installation Style:**
    *   **Highly Versatile:** RAM offers a multitude of base options:
        *   **Drill-Down (Permanent):** Most secure. Ideal for fleet vehicles where a permanent fixture is desired. Requires drilling into the dashboard or console.
        *   **No-Drill Vehicle Specific Bases:** These bolt into existing seat rail or dashboard bolts, offering a very secure, semi-permanent solution without drilling. Highly recommended for shared vehicles if available for your specific model.
        *   **Suction Cup (Heavy Duty):** While RAM's suction cups are excellent, they are generally not recommended for shared vehicles due to the ease of removal and potential for theft.
        *   **C-Clamp/U-Bolt:** Can be used on roll bars or other existing structures, but less common in standard delivery vehicles.
    *   **Modular:** The "RAM Mounts" system uses a ball-and-socket design. You choose a base, an arm (various lengths), and a device holder. This modularity allows for precise positioning and future upgrades.
*   **Locking Mechanism:**
    *   **RAM Locking Knob (RAM-KNOB3LU):** This replaces the standard tightening knob with a key-locking version. It prevents unauthorized loosening of the arm, securing the phone holder in place.
    *   **RAM X-Grip with Locking Knob (e.g., RAM-HOL-UN7BU with RAM-KNOB3LU):** The X-Grip itself is a very secure holder, and adding the locking knob to the arm makes it virtually impossible to remove the phone or the holder without the key.
    *   **RAM Tab-Lock/Keyed Lock Holders:** For tablets or larger phones, RAM offers specific holders with integrated keyed locks (e.g., RAM-HOL-TABL30U for tablets, some larger phone holders).
*   **Tradeoffs:**
    *   **Cost:** Significantly more expensive than generic mounts.
    *   **Installation Complexity:** Drill-down or no-drill bases require more effort to install initially.
    *   **Key Management:** If using keyed locks, fleet managers need to manage keys for each vehicle/mount.
    *   **Bulkiness:** RAM mounts are robust, which means they are generally larger and more industrial-looking than consumer-grade mounts.
