# Best Tablet Mounts for 2024

Here are the top tablet mount options ranked by performance, durability, and versatility:

## 1. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Series**
**Best Overall for Professional/Commercial Use**
- **Durability**: Marine-grade aluminum construction, vibration dampening
- **Installation**: Multiple base options (suction cup, permanent mount, clamp)
- **Commercial Use**: Ideal for vehicles, boats, industrial equipment, POS systems
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost ($80-150), but exceptional build quality and lifetime warranty
- **Why #1**: Proven in demanding environments, adjustable for 7"-12" tablets

## 2. **Arkon Heavy Duty Tablet Mount**
**Best Value for Commercial Applications**
- **Durability**: Steel construction with adjustable arms
- **Installation**: Drill base, clamp, or weighted base options
- **Commercial Use**: Perfect for retail counters, kiosks, restaurants
- **Tradeoffs**: Bulkier design, limited adjustability vs. RAM
- **Price**: $40-80 range
- **Why #2**: Solid commercial-grade build at lower cost than RAM

## 3. **Lamicall Adjustable Tablet Stand**
**Best Desktop/Counter Option**
- **Durability**: Aluminum alloy, supports up to 13" tablets
- **Installation**: Freestanding desktop design
- **Commercial Use**: Reception desks, point-of-sale, presentations
- **Tradeoffs**: Not suitable for mobile/vehicle use, limited to flat surfaces
- **Price**: $15-25
- **Why #3**: Excellent stability and adjustability for stationary use

## 4. **iKross 2-in-1 Car Tablet Mount**
**Best for Vehicle Use**
- **Durability**: ABS plastic with reinforced joints
- **Installation**: Headrest mount or cup holder base
- **Commercial Use**: Rideshare, delivery vehicles, mobile offices
- **Tradeoffs**: Plastic construction less durable than metal options
- **Price**: $25-40
- **Why #4**: Vehicle-specific design with good passenger accessibility

## 5. **Gooseneck Tablet Holder (Various Brands)**
**Most Flexible Positioning**
- **Durability**: Varies by brand; look for reinforced neck designs
- **Installation**: Clamp-on to desks, beds, counters
- **Commercial Use**: Healthcare settings, workshops, flexible workstations
- **Tradeoffs**: Neck can fatigue over time, less stable than rigid mounts
- **Price**: $20-50
- **Why #5**: Ultimate flexibility but compromises on long-term durability

## Key Selection Factors:

**For Heavy Commercial Use**: Choose RAM Mounts for reliability
**For Budget-Conscious Business**: Arkon offers good commercial features
**For Clean Desktop Setup**: Lamicall provides professional appearance
**For Vehicle Integration**: iKross designed specifically for automotive use
**For Maximum Flexibility**: Gooseneck when positioning is critical

Consider your specific tablet size, weight requirements, and mounting surface when making your final selection.
