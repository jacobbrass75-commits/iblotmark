## Key Commercial Considerations:
- **DOT Compliance:** Ensure mount doesn't obstruct windshield view (typically mount low-right)
- **Vibration Rating:** Semi trucks generate significant engine/road vibration - prioritize mounts tested for commercial vehicles
- **Phone Security:** Look for locking mechanisms to prevent theft during stops
- **Cable Management:** Consider mounts with integrated charging cable routing

**Installation Note:** All require drilling dashboard/console - measure twice, drill once. Consider professional installation to maintain truck warranty.
