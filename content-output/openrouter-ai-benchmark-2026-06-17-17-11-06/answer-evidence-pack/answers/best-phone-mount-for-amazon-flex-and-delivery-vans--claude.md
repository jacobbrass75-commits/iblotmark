- **Installation:** Strong suction cup base that works on dashboards and windshields; no permanent modifications needed
- **Commercial use:** Trusted by fleet operators, adjustable arms accommodate phones with cases

## 4. **Arkon Heavy Duty Truck Phone Mount**
**Why it's #4:** Purpose-built for delivery vehicles
- **Durability:** Specifically designed for commercial truck/van environments
- **Installation:** Clamps to air vents or mounts to dashboard

## Key Considerations for Delivery Use:

**Essential Features:**
- One-handed phone insertion/removal
- Secure hold during sudden stops and turns
- Clear GPS visibility without blocking windshield view
- Compatibility with phone cases (most delivery drivers use protective cases)

**Installation Tips:**
- Avoid permanent modifications to rental/company vehicles
- Position to comply with local hands-free driving laws
- Ensure mount doesn't obstruct airbags or controls

**Durability Factors:**
- Temperature resistance (hot vans in summer)
- Vibration resistance from constant driving
- Easy cleaning (gets dusty/dirty quickly)

The RAM Mount is the clear winner for serious delivery work despite the higher cost - its reliability pays for itself by preventing phone drops and mount failures during busy delivery schedules.
