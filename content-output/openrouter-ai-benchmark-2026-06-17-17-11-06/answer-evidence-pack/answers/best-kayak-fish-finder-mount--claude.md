# Best Kayak Fish Finder Mount Options

Here are the top kayak fish finder mounts ranked by versatility, durability, and ease of use:

## 1. **RAM Mounts RAM-B-177-GA50U Track Ball Mount**
**Why it's #1:** Industry gold standard for adjustability and durability
- **Commercial use:** Professional guides and tournament anglers rely on RAM's locking mechanisms
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, corrosion-resistant
- **Installation:** Clamps to most kayak track systems (YakAttack, RAM tracks)
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($80-120), requires existing track system

## 2. **YakAttack Omega Pro Fish Finder Mount**
**Why it's ranked here:** Best integrated ecosystem for serious kayak fishing
- **Commercial use:** Designed specifically for kayak fishing guides who need quick setup/breakdown
- **Durability:** Glass-filled nylon construction, UV-resistant
- **Installation:** Works with YakAttack track systems, multiple adjustment points
- **Tradeoffs:** Proprietary system lock-in, premium pricing ($90-140)

## 3. **Scotty Fishfinder Mount (#0141)**
**Why it's #3:** Excellent value with proven reliability
- **Commercial use:** Popular with charter operators for cost-effectiveness
- **Durability:** High-impact plastic, 25+ year track record
- **Installation:** Clamps to gunwales 1"-2" thick, no drilling required
- **Tradeoffs:** Less adjustability than RAM, plastic construction

## 4. **Humminbird Portable Case with Suction Cup Mount**
**Why it's #4:** Best for rental kayaks and occasional use
- **Commercial use:** Ideal for kayak rental operations (no permanent modification)
- **Durability:** Adequate for recreational use, not heavy-duty commercial
- **Installation:** Suction cup to any smooth surface, completely portable
- **Tradeoffs:** Less secure in rough conditions, limited to smaller units

## Key Installation Considerations:
- **Track-mounted systems** offer best stability but require compatible kayak
- **Clamp mounts** work on most kayaks but may stress gunwales over time
- **Suction mounts** are most versatile but least secure in choppy water

## Durability Factors:
- Saltwater use demands marine-grade materials (aluminum/stainless steel hardware)
- UV exposure requires quality plastics or anodized metals
- Vibration resistance is crucial for sonar performance

The RAM Track Ball mount wins for most serious applications due to its bombproof construction and infinite adjustability, while Scotty offers the best value for recreational users.
