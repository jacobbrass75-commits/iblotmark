# Best Barcode Scanner Mounts for Forklifts

Here are the top forklift barcode scanner mount options, ranked by reliability and commercial performance:

## 1. **RAM Mounts Forklift Scanner Mount (RAM-VB-196-SW1)**
**Why it's #1:** Industry standard with proven durability
- **Commercial use:** Warehousing, manufacturing, logistics operations
- **Durability:** Military-grade aluminum construction, vibration dampening
- **Installation:** Clamps to overhead guard or dashboard, no drilling required
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($150-250) but exceptional longevity

## 2. **Gamber-Johnson Forklift Mount Series**
**Why it's ranked here:** Heavy-duty construction for demanding environments
- **Commercial use:** Distribution centers, cold storage facilities
- **Durability:** Steel construction, sealed bearings, tested to 3G vibration
- **Installation:** Bolt-on or clamp systems available
- **Tradeoffs:** Requires more installation time but offers maximum stability

## 3. **Havis Forklift Scanner Cradle Mounts**
**Why it's competitive:** Scanner-specific designs with quick release
- **Commercial use:** Retail warehouses, cross-docking operations
- **Durability:** Powder-coated steel, shock-absorbing design
- **Installation:** Universal clamp fits most overhead guards
- **Tradeoffs:** Mid-range pricing but may require scanner-specific cradles

## 4. **Zebra VC80 Vehicle Mount Kit**
**Why it's included:** Integrated solution for Zebra scanners
- **Commercial use:** Best for operations already using Zebra ecosystem
- **Durability:** Purpose-built for vehicle environments
- **Installation:** Plug-and-play with Zebra devices
- **Tradeoffs:** Limited to Zebra scanners only, proprietary system

## Key Selection Factors:
- **Vibration resistance** is critical for forklift environments
- **Quick-release mechanisms** improve operator efficiency
- **Adjustable positioning** accommodates different operator heights
- **Cable management** prevents damage from snagging

Choose RAM Mounts for maximum durability, Gamber-Johnson for extreme conditions, or Havis for balanced performance and cost.
