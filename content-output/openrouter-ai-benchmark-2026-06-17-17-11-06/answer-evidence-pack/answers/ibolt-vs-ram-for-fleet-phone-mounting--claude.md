## Key Comparison Factors

### **Durability & Longevity**
- **RAM**: Superior shock absorption, tested to military specs (MIL-STD-810G)
- **iBolt**: Good consumer-grade durability, but less proven in harsh commercial environments

### **Installation Styles**
- **RAM**: Dashboard drilling, suction cup, or no-drill bases available
- **iBolt**: Primarily adhesive-based or lighter mounting options
- Both offer windshield and vent mounting alternatives

### **Device Compatibility**
- **RAM**: Universal cradles fit phones 4"-6.5", plus tablet options
- **iBolt**: Device-specific and universal options, strong magnetic solutions

### **Total Cost of Ownership**
- **RAM**: Higher upfront cost (~$80-150/unit) but longer lifespan
- **iBolt**: Lower initial cost (~$40-80/unit) but may need earlier replacement

## Fleet-Specific Recommendations

**Choose RAM if:**
- Heavy-duty vehicles (trucks, construction, emergency services)
- Devices stay mounted 8+ hours daily
- Multi-year deployment planned
- Budget allows for premium solution

**Choose iBolt if:**
- Light commercial use (sales, real estate)
- Frequent device removal needed
- Large fleet requiring cost optimization
