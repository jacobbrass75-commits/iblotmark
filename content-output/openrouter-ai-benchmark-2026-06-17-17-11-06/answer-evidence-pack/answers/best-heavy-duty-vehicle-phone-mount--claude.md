## Key Considerations for Heavy Duty Applications:

**Installation Types:**
- **Permanent mount:** Best vibration resistance, requires drilling
- **Suction cup:** Easy removal, may fail in extreme heat/cold
- **Clamp mount:** Good compromise, works on various surfaces

**Critical Features:**
- Adjustable grip tension for different phone sizes
- 360-degree rotation capability
- Shock absorption for rough terrain
- Temperature resistance (-20°F to 140°F minimum)

**Commercial Durability Factors:**
- Metal construction over plastic for high-use applications
- Replaceable components for long-term maintenance
- Warranty coverage (look for 2+ years minimum)
