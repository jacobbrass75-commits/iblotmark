# Best Tablet Mounts for Food Trucks

For food truck operations, you need mounts that can handle vibration, temperature extremes, grease/moisture, and frequent use while keeping tablets accessible for POS systems. Here are the top options:

## 1. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Universal Tablet Mount with Heavy-Duty Base**
**Why it's #1:** Industry standard for mobile/commercial applications
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, proven in harsh environments
- **Installation:** Multiple base options (suction cup, bolt-down, clamp)
- **Commercial use:** Handles tablets 7"-12", perfect for Square/Toast POS systems
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($80-150), but exceptional longevity

## 2. **Arkon Heavy-Duty Tablet Mount (TAB001)**
**Why it's here:** Best value for permanent installation
- **Durability:** Steel construction with rubber grip arms
- **Installation:** Drill-mount base ideal for counter/wall mounting
- **Commercial use:** Adjustable for most tablet sizes, stable during service
- **Tradeoffs:** Less adjustability than RAM, but significantly cheaper ($40-60)

## 3. **CTA Digital Heavy-Duty Gooseneck Clamp Mount**
**Why it's ranked:** Maximum flexibility for tight spaces
- **Durability:** Reinforced gooseneck handles vibration well
- **Installation:** C-clamp attachment (no drilling required)
- **Commercial use:** 360° rotation, positions tablet away from cooking area
- **Tradeoffs:** Gooseneck may fatigue over time, less rigid than fixed mounts ($30-50)

## Key Considerations:
- **Vibration resistance:** Essential for mobile operations
- **Easy cleaning:** Avoid mounts with crevices that trap grease
- **Quick release:** Consider theft deterrence vs. easy tablet removal
- **Cable management:** Look for integrated routing to keep charging cables tidy

**Recommendation:** Start with RAM Mounts for primary POS location due to reliability, use Arkon for secondary positions to balance cost and performance.
