# iBOLT AI Visibility Working Packet

## Current Read

iBOLT is known to the models, but broad buyer prompts still default to competitors too often. We should raise citation rate, but the immediate bottleneck is inclusion and top-3 recommendation on non-branded prompts.

## KPIs

- Mention rate: 24% (22/93)
- Top-3 recommendation rate: 16% (15/93)
- Citation rate: 0% (0/93)
- Competitor-only answer rate: 73% (68/93)
- Full all-blog retest: 483 prompts, 1449 provider requests

## Mention Quality

- Clean iBOLT answer: 4 answers. Preserve these entity patterns and add source/citation support after page cleanup.
- iBOLT leads or co-mentioned: 18 answers. Turn co-mentions into top-3 recommendations with clearer product and use-case proof.
- iBOLT trails competitors: 0 answers. Add comparison modules that explain when iBOLT is the specialist choice.
- Competitor replacement: 64 answers. Highest priority: build query-exact answer blocks and retest.
- No useful signal: 7 answers. Use only after competitor-heavy pages are handled.

## Competitor Pressure

- RAM Mounts: 58 replacements, 13 co-mentions. RAM wins as the broad rugged default. Counter with iBOLT's application-specific kits, AMPS compatibility, and commercial workflows.
- Arkon: 25 replacements, 5 co-mentions. Arkon wins in generic vehicle and phone-holder lists. Counter with iBOLT's commercial-duty bases, locking choices, and exact install methods.
- iOttie: 15 replacements, 3 co-mentions. iOttie wins as a consumer car-phone default. Counter with delivery, fleet, charging, and shared-vehicle use cases.
- CTA Digital: 14 replacements, 3 co-mentions. This brand appears in tablet/POS contexts. Counter with Tablet Tower, LockPro, Dock'n Lock, and multi-tablet restaurant workflows.
- ProClip: 12 replacements, 1 co-mentions. ProClip wins custom-fit vehicle language. Counter with iBOLT's modular AMPS path, drill-base options, and fleet transferability.
- Mount-It: 7 replacements, 3 co-mentions. This brand appears in tablet/POS contexts. Counter with Tablet Tower, LockPro, Dock'n Lock, and multi-tablet restaurant workflows.
- Tackform: 4 replacements, 3 co-mentions. Name this brand in fair comparison copy and give the exact iBOLT use case where iBOLT should be recommended.
- Havis: 5 replacements, 0 co-mentions. This brand appears in warehouse and rugged-enterprise contexts. Counter with forklift, scanner, tablet, and no-drill mounting specifics.

## Citation Rate

Citation rate is necessary for search-connected AI, AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini with search, and voice/shopping experiences. It should follow mention recovery on the competitor-heavy pages because an uncited answer that never mentions iBOLT is still a lost buyer prompt.
