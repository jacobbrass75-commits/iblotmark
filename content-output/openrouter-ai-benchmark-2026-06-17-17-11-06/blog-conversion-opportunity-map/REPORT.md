## Should We Chase Citation Rate?

Yes, but not by itself. The current target-domain citation baseline is 0%; the stronger near-term KPI is to raise non-branded mentions and top-3 recommendations first, then citations should follow when pages are structured as useful sources. Citation work matters most for Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini with search, and other answer engines that expose source links.
