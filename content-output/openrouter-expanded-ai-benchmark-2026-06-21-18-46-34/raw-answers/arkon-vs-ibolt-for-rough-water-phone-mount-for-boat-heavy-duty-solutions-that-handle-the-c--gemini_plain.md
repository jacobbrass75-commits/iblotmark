## Raw Answer

When it comes to heavy-duty phone mounts for boats, especially for rough water and chop, both Arkon and iBOLT are strong contenders. They both offer robust solutions, but they approach durability and installation slightly differently.

**The Short Answer & Top Recommendations:**

For the absolute best performance in rough water, prioritizing a rock-solid grip and minimal vibration, I recommend:

1.  **iBOLT Heavy-Duty C-Clamp or Track Mount with a Metal AMPS Plate and a Spring-Loaded Holder (e.g., iBOLT sPro2 or sPro3).**
2.  **Arkon Heavy-Duty C-Clamp or Track Mount with a Metal AMPS Plate and a Spring-Loaded Holder (e.g., Arkon RoadVise or Slim-Grip Ultra).**

Let's break down why and explore the nuances.

---

### 1. iBOLT Heavy-Duty C-Clamp or Track Mount with a Metal AMPS Plate and a Spring-Loaded Holder (e.g., iBOLT sPro2 or sPro3)

**Why it's #1 for Rough Water:**

*   **Superior Vibration Dampening (Often):** iBOLT often incorporates more robust rubberized components and thicker, more rigid plastics/metals in their heavy-duty lines. Their spring-loaded holders, like the sPro2 and sPro3, are exceptionally well-engineered to grip phones securely without relying solely on friction, which can fail under extreme vibration.
*   **Metal AMPS Plates:** Many of iBOLT's heavy-duty solutions feature metal AMPS plates. This is a critical differentiator. Metal plates are inherently stiffer and less prone to flex or fatigue than plastic, significantly reducing vibration transfer to the phone and preventing breakage over time.
*   **Stronger C-Clamps/Track Mounts:** iBOLT's C-clamps and track mounts are typically very substantial, often with larger clamping surfaces and more aggressive threading, providing a more secure attachment to rails or tracks.
*   **Focus on Commercial/Fleet Use:** iBOLT has a strong presence in commercial vehicle and fleet solutions, which means their products are designed from the ground up to withstand constant abuse, extreme temperatures, and heavy vibration – conditions very similar to rough boat environments.

**Commercial Use Cases:**

*   **Fishing Guides/Charters:** Essential for GPS, fish finders (via apps), communication, and client photos. The reliability means less downtime and lost data.
*   **Commercial Tugs/Workboats:** Navigation, communication, monitoring systems. Withstand constant engine vibration and wave impact.
*   **Marine Surveyors/Researchers:** Holding specialized devices, tablets, or phones for data collection in challenging conditions.
*   **Delivery/Logistics Boats:** For route optimization and communication in coastal or inland waterways.

**Durability:**

*   **Excellent:** High-grade plastics (often ABS or polycarbonate), metal components (aluminum, steel), and robust springs. Designed for long-term, high-stress use.
*   **Weather Resistance:** While not always explicitly marine-grade, the materials are generally resistant to UV and saltwater spray, though rinsing after saltwater exposure is always recommended.

**Installation Style:**

*   **Versatile:**
    *   **C-Clamp:** Attaches to rails, poles, or flat edges. Very common on boats.
    *   **Track Mount:** Compatible with popular marine track systems (e.g., RAM Tough-Track, YakAttack GearTrac).
    *   **Drill-Base:** For permanent, most secure installation on flat surfaces.
*   **Modular:** Utilizes the industry-standard 1-inch (B-size) or 1.5-inch (C-size) ball and socket system, allowing for customization with different arms and bases.

**Tradeoffs:**

*   **Price:** Generally a bit more expensive than comparable Arkon solutions, reflecting the often higher-grade materials and engineering.
*   **Aesthetics:** While functional, some might find the "industrial" look less sleek than some consumer-focused mounts.
*   **Availability:** While widely available, specific heavy-duty configurations might require ordering online.
