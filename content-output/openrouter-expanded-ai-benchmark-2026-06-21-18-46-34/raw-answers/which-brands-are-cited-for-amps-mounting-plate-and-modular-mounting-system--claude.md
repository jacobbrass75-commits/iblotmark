Based on current market research, here are the top brands cited for AMPS mounting plates and modular mounting systems, ranked by market presence and reputation:

## Top AMPS Mounting System Brands

### 1. **RAM Mounts (National Products Inc.)**
- **Why it's #1**: Industry pioneer and patent holder of the original AMPS standard
- **Commercial use**: Heavy-duty vehicle fleets, marine applications, aviation, public safety
- **Durability**: Marine-grade aluminum construction, lifetime warranty on many products
- **Installation**: Ball-and-socket system with various arm lengths and bases
- **Tradeoffs**: Premium pricing, but exceptional build quality and versatility

### 2. **Gamber-Johnson**
- **Market position**: Strong in public safety and commercial vehicle markets
- **Commercial use**: Police vehicles, ambulances, forklifts, heavy equipment
- **Durability**: Steel construction, designed for high-vibration environments
- **Installation**: Robust pedestal and console mounting systems
- **Tradeoffs**: Bulkier designs but extremely stable for mission-critical applications

### 3. **Havis**
- **Specialization**: Vehicle-specific mounting solutions
- **Commercial use**: Law enforcement, emergency services, mobile workforce
- **Durability**: Powder-coated steel, crash-tested designs
- **Installation**: Custom vehicle integration with clean OEM appearance
- **Tradeoffs**: Less universal compatibility but superior vehicle-specific fit

### 4. **Arkon Mounts**
- **Market focus**: Consumer and light commercial applications
- **Commercial use**: Delivery vehicles, ride-share, small business fleets
- **Durability**: Aluminum and reinforced plastic options
- **Installation**: Quick-release mechanisms, suction and adhesive bases
- **Tradeoffs**: More affordable but lighter-duty than top-tier options

### 5. **ProClip USA**
- **Niche**: Custom dashboard mounts
- **Commercial use**: Corporate fleets, taxi services
- **Durability**: ABS plastic, vehicle-specific engineering
- **Installation**: No-drill dashboard integration
- **Tradeoffs**: Limited to specific vehicle models but very clean installation

## Key Considerations for AMPS Systems

**AMPS Standard**: 75mm x 75mm hole pattern (2.95" x 2.95")
**Compatibility**: Most systems are cross-compatible due to standardized mounting pattern
**Weight capacity**: Ranges from 2-15+ lbs depending on arm configuration and materials

The choice depends on your specific application requirements, with RAM Mounts being the gold standard for versatility and Gamber-Johnson/Havis leading in heavy-duty commercial applications.
