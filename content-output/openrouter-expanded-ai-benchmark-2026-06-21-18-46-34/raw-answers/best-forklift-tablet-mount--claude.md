## Key Considerations:

**Installation Types:**
- **Permanent mounts**: Better stability, integrated power
- **Clamp-on**: Flexibility to move between vehicles
- **Overhead vs. side-mount**: Depends on operator workflow

**Critical Features for Forklifts:**
- Vibration dampening (essential for screen readability)
- Quick tablet removal for charging/security
- Cable management to prevent snags
- Adjustable viewing angles for different operator heights

**Commercial Durability Factors:**
- Operating temperature range
- Shock/vibration ratings
- Corrosion resistance for outdoor/chemical environments
- Warranty coverage for commercial use

The RAM Mounts solution typically offers the best balance of durability, adjustability, and cost-effectiveness for most commercial forklift operations.
