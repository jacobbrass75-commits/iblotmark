# Best Kayak Fish Finder Mount Options

Here are the top kayak fish finder mounts ranked by versatility, durability, and user feedback:

## 1. **RAM Mounts RAM-B-132-A-238U Track Ball Mount**
**Why it's #1:** Industry-standard durability with maximum adjustability
- **Commercial use:** Professional guides and tournament anglers rely on RAM's lifetime warranty
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, tested to 50+ lbs pull force
- **Installation:** Clamps to most kayak track systems (YakAttack, Scotty compatible)
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($80-120), but eliminates wobble and provides infinite positioning

## 2. **YakAttack PanFish Portrait/Landscape Mount**
**Why it's #2:** Purpose-built for kayaks with excellent stability
- **Commercial use:** Widely used by kayak fishing guides for its reliability
- **Durability:** Glass-filled nylon construction resists UV and saltwater
- **Installation:** Direct track mount or clamp-on options, tool-free adjustment
- **Tradeoffs:** Limited to YakAttack ecosystem ($60-80), but integrates perfectly with other accessories

## 3. **Scotty Transducer Arm Mount #141**
**Why it's #3:** Best value with proven track record
- **Commercial use:** Popular with charter kayak operations due to cost-effectiveness
- **Durability:** Reinforced nylon, 20+ year proven design
- **Installation:** Clamps to gunwales 1.5"-2" thick, no drilling required
- **Tradeoffs:** Less adjustability than top options ($40-60), but rock-solid once positioned

## 4. **Humminbird MHX MSM Multi-Mount System**
**Why it's #4:** Brand-specific optimization for Humminbird units
- **Commercial use:** Ideal for fleets using Humminbird exclusively
- **Durability:** Injection-molded polymer with stainless hardware
- **Installation:** Multiple base options (clamp, track, permanent)
- **Tradeoffs:** Only works with Humminbird units ($50-70), but provides OEM fit/finish

## Key Installation Considerations:
- **Track systems** offer the most flexibility for repositioning
- **Clamp mounts** work on any kayak but may stress thin hulls
- **Through-hull mounts** are most secure but require permanent modification

## Critical Factors:
- Ensure your fish finder's AMPS hole pattern matches the mount
- Consider transducer cable routing when positioning
- Factor in paddle clearance and storage requirements
- Saltwater users should prioritize stainless steel hardware

The RAM mount system offers the best long-term value for serious anglers, while the Scotty provides excellent performance at a budget-friendly price point.
