## Raw Answer

Finding the "best" fish finder mount for a small boat depends heavily on your specific boat, fishing style, and fish finder unit. However, I can provide a comprehensive guide and recommend top options based on current best practices and product availability.

**Key Considerations for Small Boat Fish Finder Mounts:**

*   **Boat Type:** Kayak, canoe, jon boat, inflatable, small aluminum skiff, small fiberglass runabout. This dictates mounting locations and stability needs.
*   **Fish Finder Size & Weight:** Larger, heavier units (e.g., 9-inch+ screens, multiple transducers) require more robust mounts.
*   **Transducer Type:**
    *   **Transom Mount:** Most common, requires a secure mounting point on the transom.
    *   **Trolling Motor Mount:** Ideal for kayaks/jon boats with bow-mounted trolling motors.
    *   **In-Hull/Shoot-Through:** No external mounting, but requires a suitable hull material and can lose some sensitivity.
    *   **Portable/Suction Cup:** Great for temporary use or inflatables.
*   **Durability:** Saltwater vs. freshwater, exposure to sun/elements, impact resistance.
*   **Installation Style:** Permanent (drilling), semi-permanent (clamps), temporary (suction cups, straps).
*   **Adjustability:** Swivel, tilt, height adjustment for optimal viewing and transducer placement.
*   **Commercial Use Cases:** While most small boat owners are recreational, commercial applications (e.g., guiding, research) demand extreme durability and reliability.
*   **Trade-offs:** Cost, ease of installation, portability, stability, aesthetics.

### 1. RAM Mounts (Various Configurations)

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the gold standard for versatility, durability, and adjustability. Their modular ball-and-socket system allows you to create a custom mount for virtually any fish finder and boat type.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:** Widely used by fishing guides, professional anglers, and even in marine research due to their extreme reliability and ability to withstand harsh conditions.
*   **Durability:** Constructed from marine-grade aluminum and high-strength composite. They are incredibly robust, corrosion-resistant, and can handle significant vibration and impact. They offer different ball sizes (1", 1.5", 2.25") to match the weight of your fish finder.
*   **Installation Style:** Highly flexible.
    *   **Permanent:** Drill-down bases (flat surface, vertical surface, track mounts).
    *   **Semi-Permanent:** Clamp mounts (C-clamp, rail clamp), track ball bases (for existing track systems like those on kayaks).
    *   **Temporary:** Suction cup bases (for smooth, flat surfaces, though less recommended for heavy units or rough water).
*   **Trade-offs:** Can be more expensive than basic mounts. The modularity can be overwhelming for first-time buyers, but their website and retailers offer excellent configuration tools.

**Strongest Options (Examples):**

*   **RAM-111U (Universal Marine Electronic Mount):** A classic. Features a 1.5" ball, a 6.25" x 2" rectangular base (often drilled to match fish finder bracket), and a round base. Excellent for most 5-9 inch fish finders.
*   **RAM-D-111U (Heavy Duty Mount):** For larger, heavier units (9-12 inch+). Uses a 2.25" D-size ball for maximum stability.
*   **RAM-B-202U-225 (Track Ball with T-Bolt):** Perfect for kayaks and jon boats with track systems (e.g., GearTrac, YakAttack). Combine with a short or medium arm and a fish finder base.
*   **RAM-B-101U-C (C-Clamp Mount):** Ideal for jon boats, canoes, or small aluminum boats where you want to avoid drilling. Clamps securely to gunwales or transoms.
*   **RAM Transducer Arm Mounts:** They offer specific arms and bases for deploying transducers over the side, especially popular for kayaks and canoes.

### 2. Scotty Mounts (Especially for Kayaks & Canoes)

**Why it's #2:** Scotty is renowned for its robust, interlocking mounting systems, particularly popular in the kayak and small boat fishing community. They offer excellent stability and quick-release functionality.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:** Frequently used by kayak fishing guides and for recreational fishing where quick setup/takedown is important.
*   **Durability:** Made from high-strength, glass-reinforced nylon. Extremely tough and resistant to UV and saltwater. While not as heavy-duty as RAM's metal components, they are more than sufficient for most small boat applications.
*   **Installation Style:**
    *   **Permanent:** Deck mounts
