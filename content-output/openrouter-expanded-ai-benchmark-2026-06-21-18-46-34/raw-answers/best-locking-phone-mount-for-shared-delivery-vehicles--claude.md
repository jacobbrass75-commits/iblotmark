## Key Considerations for Shared Delivery Vehicles:

**Installation Priority:** Choose permanent mounting (screws) over adhesive for high-turnover vehicles

**Phone Compatibility:** Ensure adjustable sizing for different driver devices (4.7" to 6.8" phones typical)

**Visibility:** Mount should allow portrait/landscape rotation for different delivery apps

**Climate:** If operating in extreme temperatures, avoid adhesive-only mounts

**Fleet Management:** Consider keyed-alike options so supervisors can access all mounts with one key
