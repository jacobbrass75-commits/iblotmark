## Key Considerations:

**Installation Types**:
- **Transom mount**: Easiest, good for most recreational use
- **Thru-hull**: Best performance, requires professional installation
- **In-hull**: Good compromise, no hull penetration needed

**Commercial Tradeoffs**:
- Higher-end units offer better warranty support and durability
- Consider networking capabilities for multiple display setups
- Factor in transducer upgrade costs for optimal performance

**Screen Size Impact**:
- 7" minimum recommended for serious fishing
- 9"+ better for split-screen viewing and detailed charts
- Larger screens consume more power

The Garmin ECHOMAP UHD2 73sv wins overall due to its excellent sonar performance, reliable GPS mapping, and strong build quality at a reasonable price point suitable for both recreational and light commercial use.
