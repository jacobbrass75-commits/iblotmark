# Restaurant Tablet POS Mount Recommendations

Here are the top tablet mount solutions for restaurant POS and delivery apps, ranked by overall value and commercial suitability:

## 1. **Arkon Heavy-Duty Tablet Wall Mount (TAB001-HD22)**
**Best Overall for Fixed Stations**
- **Commercial Use**: Perfect for order stations, kitchen displays, and customer-facing kiosks
- **Durability**: Steel construction with 22mm arm, supports tablets 7-12 inches
- **Installation**: Wall-mounted with VESA-compatible mounting plate
- **Tradeoffs**: Fixed position (not adjustable once mounted), requires wall space
- **Price Range**: $80-120

## 2. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Universal Tablet Mount (RAM-B-138-UN10U)**
**Most Versatile Commercial Solution**
- **Commercial Use**: Excellent for mobile order-taking, tableside service, food trucks
- **Durability**: Marine-grade aluminum, proven in harsh environments, lifetime warranty
- **Installation**: Multiple base options (clamp, drill-down, suction)
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost, may be overkill for stationary use
- **Price Range**: $150-250

## 3. **CTA Digital Security Kiosk Stand (PAD-ASKM)**
**Best for Customer Self-Service**
- **Commercial Use**: Ideal for self-ordering kiosks, customer check-in stations
- **Durability**: Heavy steel base (15+ lbs), lockable enclosure prevents theft
- **Installation**: Freestanding, no mounting required
- **Tradeoffs**: Takes floor space, less flexible positioning
- **Price Range**: $200-300

## 4. **Heckler Design WindFall Stand**
**Premium Counter Solution**
- **Commercial Use**: High-end restaurants, customer payment stations
- **Durability**: Cast zinc construction, designed for 24/7 commercial use
- **Installation**: Weighted base for countertops, clean aesthetic
- **Tradeoffs**: Most expensive option, limited to specific tablet models
- **Price Range**: $300-400

## 5. **Tablet Mount Guys Swing Arm Wall Mount**
**Budget-Friendly Adjustable Option**
- **Commercial Use**: Kitchen displays, staff stations where flexibility needed
- **Durability**: Aluminum construction, good for light-medium commercial use
- **Installation**: Wall-mounted with adjustable swing arm
- **Tradeoffs**: Lower weight capacity, may not withstand heavy daily use
- **Price Range**: $60-90

## Key Considerations for Restaurant Use:

**For Kitchen Displays**: Wall mounts (#1, #5) work best to save counter space
**For Mobile Order-Taking**: RAM Mounts (#2) offers maximum flexibility
**For Customer Self-Service**: Security stands (#3, #4) prevent theft and damage
**For Payment Processing**: Weighted stands allow easy customer interaction

**Installation Tips**: Ensure mounts accommodate your specific tablet model and consider cable management for charging. Most commercial operations benefit from theft-deterrent features and easy cleaning surfaces.
