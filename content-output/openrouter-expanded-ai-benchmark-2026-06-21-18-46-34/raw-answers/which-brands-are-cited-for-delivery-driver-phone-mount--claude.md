## Raw Answer

Based on current market research and professional driver feedback, here are the top phone mount brands specifically recommended for delivery drivers:

## Top Ranked Options:

### 1. **RAM Mounts**
- **Why it's #1**: Industry standard for commercial vehicles
- **Commercial use**: Used by UPS, FedEx, and fleet operators
- **Durability**: Military-grade materials, lifetime warranty on some models
- **Installation**: Ball-and-socket system allows infinite adjustment; suction cup, adhesive, or permanent mounting options
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost ($40-150+), but exceptional longevity

### 2. **ProClip USA**
- **Why it ranks high**: Custom-fit solutions for specific vehicle models
- **Commercial use**: Popular with rideshare and delivery fleets
- **Durability**: ABS plastic construction, designed for daily professional use
- **Installation**: Vehicle-specific clips that integrate with dashboard design
- **Tradeoffs**: More expensive ($60-120), requires vehicle-specific parts

### 3. **Arkon Mounts**
- **Why it's recommended**: Strong commercial focus with delivery-specific models
- **Commercial use**: Heavy-duty truck and van mounting systems
- **Durability**: Reinforced construction for high-vibration environments
- **Installation**: Multiple mounting options including seat rail and console mounts
- **Tradeoffs**: Bulkier design, moderate pricing ($25-80)

### 4. **Mob Armor**
- **Why delivery drivers choose it**: Magnetic system with strong hold
- **Commercial use**: Popular with motorcycle and bicycle delivery riders
- **Durability**: Weatherproof, shock-resistant
- **Installation**: Magnetic plate system, quick attachment/removal
- **Tradeoffs**: Requires metal plate on phone/case, strong magnets may affect some phone functions

### 5. **iOttie**
- **Why it makes the list**: Reliable consumer brand that scales to light commercial use
- **Commercial use**: Good for gig economy drivers (DoorDash, Uber Eats)
- **Durability**: Solid build quality for the price point
- **Installation**: Easy suction cup or vent mounting
- **Tradeoffs**: Less robust than commercial-grade options, better for part-time use ($20-50)

## Key Considerations for Delivery Drivers:
- **Vibration resistance** for constant driving
- **Quick phone access** for scanning/photos
- **Portrait and landscape** orientation capability
- **One-handed operation** for efficiency
- **Temperature resistance** for various climates

RAM Mounts consistently receives the highest citations from professional drivers due to its proven durability in demanding commercial environments.
