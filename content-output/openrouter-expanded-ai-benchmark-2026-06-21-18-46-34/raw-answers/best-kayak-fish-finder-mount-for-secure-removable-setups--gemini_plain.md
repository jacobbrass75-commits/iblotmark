## Top Kayak Fish Finder Mounts for Secure Removable Setups

### 1. RAM Mounts (Ball & Socket System)

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the gold standard for secure, adjustable, and highly customizable mounting solutions across various industries, including marine. Their ball and socket system offers unparalleled flexibility and vibration dampening.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:**
    *   **Fishing Guides:** Quickly reconfigure setups for different clients or fishing styles.
    *   **Rental Fleets:** Easy removal for cleaning, storage, and preventing theft.
    *   **Tournament Anglers:** Rapid deployment and breakdown, consistent positioning.
    *   **Marine Survey/Research:** Securely mount sensitive equipment that needs precise positioning.
*   **Durability:**
    *   **High:** Constructed from marine-grade aluminum (powder-coated) or high-strength composite. They are incredibly robust and resistant to UV, saltwater, and impact.
    *   **Vibration Dampening:** The rubber ball design inherently absorbs shock and vibration, protecting your electronics.
*   **Installation Style:**
    *   **Base Plate:** Requires a base plate to be permanently mounted to your kayak. Common options include:
        *   **RAM 1.5" or 2.25" Ball Base:** Screws directly into the kayak (often with backing plates for reinforcement).
        *   **RAM Track Ball:** Slides into a gear track (e.g., YakAttack GearTrac, Hobie H-Rail). This is the most popular removable option.
        *   **RAM Suction Cup Base:** For temporary, non-drilling applications on smooth, flat surfaces (less secure for rough water).
    *   **Arm & Ball:** An arm connects the base ball to another ball attached to your fish finder's bracket. Arms come in various lengths.
    *   **Fish Finder Specific Plate:** RAM offers plates designed to fit popular fish finder brands (Garmin, Lowrance, Humminbird, etc.), which then attach to the ball.
*   **Removability:**
    *   **Excellent:** Loosen the single knob on the arm, and the entire arm, fish finder, and transducer pole (if using a RAM transducer arm) can be removed in seconds. The base plate remains.
*   **Tradeoffs:**
    *   **Cost:** Generally more expensive than simpler mounts.
    *   **Bulk:** Can be a bit bulkier than low-profile options, especially with longer arms.
    *   **Permanent Base:** While the fish finder is removable, the base plate itself is usually permanently screwed into the kayak (unless using a track ball).

**Recommended RAM Components for a Removable Setup:**

*   **Base:**
    *   **RAM Track Ball (RAP-B-354U-TRA1):** If your kayak has a gear track.
    *   **RAM 1.5" Ball Base (RAM-202U):** If you need to screw directly into the kayak.
*   **Arm:**
    *   **RAM Double Socket Arm (RAM-B-201U-C):** Standard length for most setups.
*   **Fish Finder Plate:**
    *   **RAM Universal Marine Electronic Mount (RAM-111U):** Fits most fish finder gimbal brackets.
    *   **Brand-Specific Plate:** Check RAM's website for plates tailored to your specific fish finder model for a cleaner fit.
*   **Transducer Mount (Optional but Recommended for Removability):**
    *   **RAM Transducer Arm (RAM-B-316-18-TRA1U):** Attaches to a track ball and allows for easy deployment and retrieval of your transducer.
