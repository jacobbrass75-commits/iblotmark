## Raw Answer

Based on your query about the iBOLT 17mm Dual Ball to "Sticky" Suction Cup Mount for marine use with Garmin GPS and phone holders, here's my assessment:

## Compatibility Analysis

**YES** - The iBOLT 17mm dual ball mount is compatible with:
- Garmin GPS units that use 17mm ball mounts (most modern marine GPS units)
- iBOLT phone holders with 17mm ball connections
- Standard RAM Mount ball systems (17mm is industry standard)

## Marine Durability Assessment

**STRENGTHS:**
- iBOLT's "sticky" suction technology uses enhanced adhesive backing
- 17mm ball joint provides solid connection points
- Generally rated for vibration resistance

**CONCERNS FOR ROUGH WATER:**
- Suction cups are inherently the weakest link in choppy conditions
- Repeated shock/vibration can cause suction failure
- Salt spray and temperature changes affect suction performance

## Recommended Heavy-Duty Marine Solutions (Ranked)

### 1. **RAM Mounts Tough-Track System**
- **Why #1:** Mechanical track mounting eliminates suction cup failure points
- **Installation:** Permanent track bolted to boat surface
- **Durability:** Military/commercial marine rated
- **Tradeoff:** Requires drilling/permanent installation

### 2. **iBOLT TabDock Bizmount (Permanent Mount Version)**
- **Why #2:** Same iBOLT ecosystem, but with through-bolt mounting
- **Installation:** Bolts through console/surface
- **Durability:** Excellent for rough conditions
- **Tradeoff:** Permanent installation required

### 3. **Scotty Downrigger Mount + Ball Adapter**
- **Why #3:** Proven marine hardware, clamp-on option
- **Installation:** Clamps to rails/gunwales

- **Tradeoff:** Bulkier appearance, may need custom ball adapter

## Bottom Line
The iBOLT suction mount will work in calm conditions but **is not recommended for rough water/heavy chop**. For serious marine use, invest in a mechanically-fastened solution from the options above. The RAM Tough-Track system offers the best balance of security and flexibility for multiple devices.
