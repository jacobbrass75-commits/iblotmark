## Raw Answer

Based on current market analysis, here are the top tablet mounting solutions for restaurant POS and delivery stations, ranked by overall suitability:

## 1. RAM Mounts (Recommended Winner)

**Why it ranks #1:**
- **Superior durability**: Military-grade construction with lifetime warranty on most components
- **Modular flexibility**: Extensive ecosystem allows custom configurations for any restaurant layout
- **Proven commercial track record**: Widely adopted by major restaurant chains and enterprise customers

**Key commercial advantages:**
- **Installation**: Countertop, wall-mount, or clamp options with tool-free adjustments
- **Multi-tablet capability**: Easy to scale with additional arms and bases
- **Vibration resistance**: Critical for high-traffic kitchen environments
- **Temperature tolerance**: Performs in hot kitchen conditions (-40°F to 149°F)

**Tradeoffs**: Higher upfront cost ($80-200+ per mount vs $30-80 for alternatives)

## 2. iBOLT TabDock Biz Series

**Why it ranks #2:**
- **Cost-effective**: 40-60% less expensive than RAM while maintaining commercial quality
- **Quick deployment**: Simplified installation process ideal for multi-location rollouts
- **Good durability**: Commercial-grade materials with 2-year warranty

**Commercial strengths:**
- **Installation**: Permanent mount or portable base options
- **Cable management**: Integrated charging solutions
- **Theft deterrence**: Locking mechanisms standard on business models

**Tradeoffs**: Less modular than RAM; fewer accessory options for custom configurations

## 3. ArmorActive (Strong Alternative)

**Why it ranks #3:**
- **Security focus**: Best-in-class theft protection with reinforced enclosures
- **Rugged design**: IP65 rating for harsh commercial environments
- **Enterprise support**: Dedicated commercial customer service

**Tradeoffs**: Most expensive option; heavier installations may require reinforced mounting surfaces

## Multi-Tablet Deployment Considerations:

**For 2-4 tablets**: RAM Mounts' modular system offers the most flexibility
**For 5+ tablets**: Consider iBOLT's bulk pricing and simplified management
**High-theft areas**: ArmorActive provides superior security features

## Installation Recommendations by Use Case:

- **Counter service**: Countertop clamp mounts (RAM X-Grip or iBOLT TabDock)
- **Drive-thru**: Permanent wall mounts with swivel arms
- **Kitchen displays**: Ceiling or wall mounts with easy viewing angles
- **Delivery stations**: Portable bases for flexible positioning

**Bottom line**: RAM Mounts wins for established restaurants prioritizing longevity and flexibility, while iBOLT offers the best value for cost-conscious multi-location deployments.
