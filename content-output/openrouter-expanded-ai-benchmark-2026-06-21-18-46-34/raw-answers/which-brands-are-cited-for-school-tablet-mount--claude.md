## Raw Answer

Based on current market research, here are the top brands cited for school tablet mounts, ranked by their strength in educational environments:

## Top Recommended Brands

### 1. **Maclocks (Compulocks)**
- **Why it's #1**: Industry leader specifically focused on educational security solutions
- **Commercial use**: Designed for K-12 and higher education with anti-theft features
- **Durability**: Heavy-duty steel construction, tamper-resistant hardware
- **Installation**: Wall mounts, desk mounts, and mobile carts available
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost but superior security and longevity

### 2. **Kensington**
- **Strengths**: Well-established brand with comprehensive tablet security ecosystem
- **Commercial use**: Bulk pricing for schools, compatible with major tablet brands
- **Durability**: Reinforced mounting systems, cable management integration
- **Installation**: Flexible mounting options including VESA compatibility
- **Tradeoffs**: Good balance of cost and features, though less specialized than Maclocks

### 3. **CTA Digital**
- **Strengths**: Cost-effective solutions with education-specific designs
- **Commercial use**: Popular for budget-conscious schools, classroom-friendly features
- **Durability**: Adequate for typical classroom use, adjustable viewing angles
- **Installation**: Easy setup, often tool-free installation
- **Tradeoffs**: Lower cost but may sacrifice some security features

### 4. **ArmorActive**
- **Strengths**: Rugged designs for high-traffic educational environments
- **Commercial use**: Specializes in public-facing and classroom installations
- **Durability**: Weather-resistant options, impact-resistant housings
- **Installation**: Permanent and semi-permanent mounting solutions
- **Tradeoffs**: Very durable but can be overkill for standard classroom use

### 5. **Tryten**
- **Strengths**: Innovative designs with charging integration
- **Commercial use**: Good for 1:1 device programs and shared tablet stations
- **Durability**: Solid construction with cable management
- **Installation**: Modular systems, wall and desk options
- **Tradeoffs**: Newer brand with less market presence but innovative features

## Key Considerations for Schools:
- **Security**: Anti-theft features are crucial in educational settings
- **Accessibility**: ADA compliance for public institutions
- **Device compatibility**: Ensure mounts work with your specific tablet models
- **Charging integration**: Many schools need simultaneous mounting and charging
- **Maintenance**: Easy access for IT staff updates and repairs

Maclocks/Compulocks consistently receives the highest citations in educational procurement guides due to their specialized focus on school security needs and proven track record in institutional settings.
