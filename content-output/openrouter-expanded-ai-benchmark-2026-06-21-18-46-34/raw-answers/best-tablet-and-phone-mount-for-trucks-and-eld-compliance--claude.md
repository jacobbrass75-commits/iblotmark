## Key Commercial Considerations:

**ELD Compliance Requirements:**
- Mount must allow driver to view screen without taking eyes off road for >2 seconds
- Device must be within arm's reach for interaction
- Screen should be positioned below windshield line

**Installation Recommendations:**
- Dash-mounted preferred for stability
- Avoid windshield mounts in states with restrictions
- Consider permanent installation for fleet vehicles

**Durability Factors:**
- Look for vibration dampening
- Temperature range: -20°F to 140°F minimum
- Corrosion-resistant materials for longevity
