1.  **High Security (Locking Mechanism):** This is the LockPro's biggest selling point. The tablet is securely encased and locked, preventing theft or unauthorized removal. 3.  **Permanent, Secure Installation (Drill Base):** The drill base ensures the stand is firmly attached to a counter, table, or wall. This prevents the tablet from being knocked over, moved, or stolen. For POS, this means a stable interface for transactions.
