## Key Considerations for Construction/Work Trucks:

**Essential Features:**
- Shock/vibration resistance for rough terrain
- Easy one-handed phone insertion/removal with work gloves
- 360-degree rotation for landscape/portrait viewing
- Charging cable management
- Theft deterrence (locking mechanisms)

**Installation Recommendations:**
- Avoid windshield suction mounts (fail in extreme temperatures/vibration)
- Consider dash-mounted or console-integrated solutions
- Ensure mount doesn't interfere with airbag deployment zones

**ELD Compliance Notes:**
Most ELD apps work with any secure mount, but verify your specific ELD provider's hardware requirements before purchasing.
