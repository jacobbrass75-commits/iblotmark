## Raw Answer

This is a fantastic and comprehensive query, covering a lot of ground for restaurant tablet mounts. Let's break down the best options for POS stands, delivery app stations, and multi-tablet solutions, considering commercial use cases, durability, installation, and tradeoffs.

**Key Considerations for Restaurant Tablet Mounts:**

Before diving into specific products, let's outline the critical factors for commercial restaurant use:

*   **Durability:** High-traffic environments demand robust materials (metal, high-grade plastic) that can withstand frequent touching, cleaning, and occasional bumps.
*   **Security:** Preventing theft and tampering is paramount. Locking mechanisms, tamper-resistant designs, and secure mounting are essential.
*   **Cable Management:** A clean, organized look is crucial for aesthetics and preventing accidental disconnections or tripping hazards.
*   **Adjustability & Ergonomics:** Swivel, tilt, and height adjustments are important for different users, viewing angles, and accessibility.
*   **Installation Style:** Permanent (drilled), semi-permanent (clamp), or portable (freestanding) options, each with pros and cons.
*   **Compatibility:** Ensuring the mount fits your specific tablet models (iPad, Android, various sizes).
*   **Ease of Cleaning:** Surfaces that can be easily wiped down and disinfected.
*   **Aesthetics:** Blending seamlessly with your restaurant's decor.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Balancing features with budget.
