## Raw Answer

Let's break down the iBOLT 17mm Dual Ball to "Sticky" Suction Cup Mount Base for your rough water boat phone mount needs, and then explore some heavy-duty alternatives.

**iBOLT 17mm Dual Ball to "Sticky" Suction Cup Mount Base with Garmin GPS and iBOLT Phone Holders**

**Compatibility:**

*   **Garmin GPS:** Yes, if your Garmin GPS has a 17mm ball socket on its cradle or a compatible adapter. Many Garmin automotive GPS units use a 17mm ball. However, some marine-specific Garmin units (especially larger chartplotters) might use a different mounting system (e.g., RAM B-ball or C-ball, or a proprietary flush mount). **Crucially, you need to verify the specific Garmin GPS model you have and its mounting interface.**
*   **iBOLT Phone Holders:** Yes, this mount is designed to work seamlessly with iBOLT phone holders that feature a 17mm ball socket. iBOLT is known for its modular 17mm ball system.

**"Good for Rough Water Phone Mount for Boat Heavy-Duty Solutions that Handle the Chop"?**

This is where we need to be realistic.

*   **"Sticky" Suction Cup:** While iBOLT's "sticky" suction cups are generally better than standard suction cups, they still rely on a vacuum seal and a slightly adhesive gel. In **rough water with significant chop, vibrations, and potential for water ingress around the seal**, **suction cups are inherently the weakest link for heavy-duty, reliable mounting.** They can lose suction, especially on textured surfaces, or if the surface gets wet or dirty.
*   **17mm Ball System:** The 17mm ball is a common and versatile size, but it's generally considered a "light to medium-duty" standard. For truly heavy-duty applications in rough marine environments, larger ball sizes (like RAM Mounts' 1" B-ball or 1.5" C-ball) offer significantly more clamping force and stability.
*   **Dual Ball Design:** The dual ball design offers good adjustability, which is a plus.

**Commercial Use Cases:**

*   **Light-duty recreational boating:** Kayaks, paddleboards, calm lake fishing boats, pontoon boats where chop is minimal.
*   **Temporary mounting:** For quick, non-critical use where you don't want to drill.
*   **Dash mounting in vehicles:** Where vibrations are less severe than a boat.

**Durability:**

*   The plastic components are generally robust for their intended use.
*   The "sticky" gel can degrade over time with exposure to UV, salt water, and extreme temperatures. It can also attract dirt.
*   The suction mechanism itself is the primary point of failure in rough conditions.

**Installation Style:**

*   **Tool-free, temporary.** You press it onto a smooth, non-porous surface and engage the lever.

**Tradeoffs:**

*   **Pros:** Easy to install/remove, highly adjustable, no drilling required, good for light-duty use.
*   **Cons:** **Not truly heavy-duty for rough water**, risk of detachment in chop, suction cup reliability issues in marine environments, 17mm ball size is less robust than larger alternatives.
