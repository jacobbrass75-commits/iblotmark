# Best Fish Finder Mounts for Small Boats

Here are the top fish finder mounting options for small boats, ranked by versatility and performance:

## 1. **RAM Mounts RAM-101U-B Ball Mount System**
**Why it's #1:** Industry gold standard for marine electronics mounting
- **Commercial use:** Professional guides and charter boats rely on these
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum, corrosion-resistant, handles heavy seas
- **Installation:** Clamp-on or bolt-down base options, no permanent modifications
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($60-120), but exceptional adjustability and longevity

## 2. **Scotty Fishfinder Mount (#135)**
**Why it's #2:** Purpose-built for fishing applications
- **Commercial use:** Popular with tournament anglers and fishing guides
- **Durability:** High-impact plastic, UV-resistant, designed for marine environment
- **Installation:** Quick-release system, mounts to gunwale or console
- **Tradeoffs:** Less adjustable than RAM mounts, but faster deployment ($40-60)

## 3. **Humminbird MC-W Mount**
**Why it's #3:** Excellent value with solid performance
- **Commercial use:** Good for recreational and light commercial use
- **Durability:** Reinforced plastic construction, adequate for most conditions
- **Installation:** Clamp-on design fits most boat rails and surfaces
- **Tradeoffs:** Brand-specific compatibility, less robust than top options ($25-40)

## 4. **Lowrance/Simrad Gimbal Bracket**
**Why it's #4:** Best for permanent installations
- **Commercial use:** Ideal for boats with dedicated electronics space
- **Durability:** Stainless steel hardware, very stable platform
- **Installation:** Requires drilling/permanent mounting to console or dash
- **Tradeoffs:** Not portable, requires commitment to mounting location ($30-50)

## Key Considerations for Small Boats:
- **Vibration dampening** is crucial for sonar performance
- **Quick-release capability** helps prevent theft and weather damage
- **Adjustable viewing angles** compensate for limited space and seating positions
- **Corrosion resistance** is non-negotiable in marine environments

The RAM Mount system offers the best long-term value despite higher upfront cost, while the Scotty mount provides the best fishing-specific features for the price.
