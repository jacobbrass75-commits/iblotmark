Based on current market analysis, here are the top tablet mounts for restaurant POS and delivery applications, ranked by overall value for commercial use:

## 1. **ArmorActive Pipeline Kiosk Stand**
**Why it's #1:** Purpose-built for commercial POS environments

- **Commercial features:** Cable management, lockable tablet enclosure, anti-theft design
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($200-400), but justified by security and durability

## 2. **CTA Digital Premium Security Kiosk Stand**
**Why it's #2:** Excellent security at mid-range pricing

- **Installation:** Freestanding with locking mechanisms
- **Commercial features:** Fits multiple tablet sizes, integrated cable routing
- **Tradeoffs:** Bulkier footprint, moderate price point ($150-250)
- **Best for:** Order stations, customer-facing displays

## 3. **RAM Mounts X-Grip with Heavy Duty Base**
**Why it's #3:** Maximum flexibility and proven reliability

- **Installation:** Multiple mounting options (clamp, bolt, suction)
- **Commercial features:** 360° rotation, adjustable tension, modular system
- **Tradeoffs:** Less theft protection, requires separate security accessories
- **Best for:** Kitchen displays, manager stations, flexible positioning needs

## 4. **Square Stand for iPad**
**Why it's #4:** Seamless integration if using Square POS

- **Installation:** Plug-and-play with Square ecosystem
- **Commercial features:** Built-in card reader, optimized for Square software

## 5. **Heckler Design WindFall Stand**
**Why it's #5:** Premium design for upscale establishments
- **Durability:** Zinc alloy construction, premium materials

- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost, less security features, limited adjustability
- **Best for:** Fine dining, customer-facing applications where aesthetics matter

## Key Considerations for Restaurant Use:

**Security Needs:** Options 1-2 offer the best theft protection for customer-accessible areas
**Flexibility:** Option 3 (RAM) provides the most mounting versatility
**Integration:** Consider your POS software compatibility
**Environment:** Kitchen mounts need easy cleaning; customer areas need tamper resistance
**Budget:** Expect $150-400 for commercial-grade solutions

For most restaurants, I'd recommend starting with the **ArmorActive Pipeline** for customer-facing stations and **RAM Mounts** for back-of-house applications where flexibility is more important than security.
