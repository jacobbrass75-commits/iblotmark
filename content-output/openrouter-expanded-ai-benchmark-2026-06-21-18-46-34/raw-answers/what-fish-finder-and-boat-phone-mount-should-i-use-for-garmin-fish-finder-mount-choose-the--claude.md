# Fish Finder and Phone Mount Setup Guide

## **Top Fish Finder Recommendations (Ranked)**

### 1. **Garmin Striker Vivid 7sv** - Best Overall Value
- **Commercial Use**: Excellent for charter fishing, guides, and serious recreational use
- **Durability**: IPX7 waterproof rating, rugged construction
- **Installation**: Transom mount transducer, simple 4-wire connection
- **Key Features**: 7" color display, CHIRP sonar, side/down imaging
- **Tradeoffs**: No GPS mapping (GPS available in Plus models)
- **Price Range**: $400-500

### 2. **Garmin ECHOMAP UHD2 73sv** - Best Premium Option
- **Commercial Use**: Professional-grade for charter boats and commercial fishing
- **Durability**: Marine-grade construction, excellent in harsh conditions
- **Installation**: Flush or bail mount options, network-capable
- **Key Features**: 7" touchscreen, preloaded maps, wireless connectivity
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost, more complex installation
- **Price Range**: $800-1000

### 3. **Garmin Striker 4** - Best Budget/Kayak Option
- **Commercial Use**: Light commercial use, perfect for kayak fishing guides
- **Durability**: Compact, lightweight, fully waterproof
- **Installation**: Portable kit available, minimal wiring
- **Key Features**: 3.5" display, CHIRP sonar, waypoint marking
- **Tradeoffs**: Smaller screen, basic features
- **Price Range**: $150-200

## **Recommended Phone Mounts (Ranked)**

### 1. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Phone Mount** - Most Versatile
- **Installation**: Clamps to rails, pedestals, or flat surfaces

- **Commercial Use**: Industry standard for charter boats
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost but exceptional reliability

## **Installation Recommendations by Boat Type**

### **Powerboat/Center Console**
- **Fish Finder**: Flush mount ECHOMAP UHD2 at helm
- **Phone Mount**: RAM Mount on console rail
- **Transducer**: Through-hull or transom mount

### **Bass Boat**
- **Fish Finder**: Striker Vivid 7sv on adjustable pedestal
- **Phone Mount**: RAM Mount with pedestal base
- **Transducer**: Transom mount with kick-up bracket

### **Kayak**
- **Fish Finder**: Striker 4 with portable kit
- **Phone Mount**: Scotty mount integrated with rod holder
- **Transducer**: Scupper hole or side-mount transducer

## **Key Installation Considerations**

**Power Requirements**: Most Garmin units need 12V power. Kayaks may need portable battery packs.

**Transducer Placement**: Critical for performance - avoid turbulent water areas, ensure proper angle.

**Screen Visibility**: Consider sun glare and viewing angles when positioning both devices.

**Cable Management**: Use marine-grade cable covers and avoid sharp edges that could damage wiring.

## **Budget Recommendations**

- **Budget Setup ($250-350)**: Striker 4 + Scotty phone mount
- **Mid-Range ($600-800)**: Striker Vivid 7sv + RAM X-Grip mount  
- **Premium ($1000+)**: ECHOMAP UHD2 + RAM mount with custom pedestal

The Striker Vivid 7sv with RAM X-Grip mount offers the best balance of features, durability, and value for most users, whether recreational or light commercial use.
