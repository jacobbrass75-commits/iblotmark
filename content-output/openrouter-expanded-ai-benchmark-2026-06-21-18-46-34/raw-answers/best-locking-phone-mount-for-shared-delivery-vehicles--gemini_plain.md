## Raw Answer

For shared delivery vehicles, a "best locking phone mount" needs to prioritize **security, durability, ease of use for multiple drivers, and adaptability to various vehicle interiors.** Given the high-turnover nature of shared vehicles, a permanent, hard-to-install solution is often not ideal.

Here's a breakdown of the best options, ranked from strongest to weakest, with detailed explanations:

---

## Top Recommendations for Locking Phone Mounts in Shared Delivery Vehicles

### 1. RAM Mounts (Various Configurations) - **Strongest Overall**

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the industry standard for rugged, modular, and highly customizable mounting solutions. Their "locking" aspect comes from the sheer strength of their components and the ability to create a very secure, vibration-resistant hold, rather than a key-lock mechanism (though those exist for specific components).

*   **Commercial Use Cases:** Ideal for any delivery service (food, package, grocery), field service technicians, emergency services, and fleet management. Their versatility means they can be adapted to almost any vehicle type.
*   **Durability:** Unmatched. Made from marine-grade aluminum, high-strength composites, and stainless steel. They are designed to withstand extreme vibrations, temperature fluctuations, and constant use. You'll replace the phone before you replace the RAM mount.
*   **Installation Style:** Highly modular.
    *   **Base:** Suction cup (heavy-duty twist-lock), drill-down (most secure, but permanent), seat rail bolt, or even a C-clamp for specific surfaces.
    *   **Arm:** Various lengths and types (standard, double socket, flex arm).
    *   **Cradle:** X-Grip (spring-loaded, universal), Quick-Grip (one-hand operation), or specific form-fit cradles for popular phone models.
*   **Tradeoffs:**
    *   **Cost:** Generally the most expensive option upfront.
    *   **"Locking" Mechanism:** While incredibly secure against accidental dislodgement, the standard X-Grip or Quick-Grip doesn't offer a physical key-lock to prevent theft of the phone itself. For theft prevention, you'd need to add a specific locking cradle (e.g., RAM-HOL-AQ7-2U for specific phones or a universal locking tablet holder if using a tablet). However, the *mount itself* is so robust it's unlikely to fail or be easily removed without tools if drill-mounted.
    *   **Installation Time:** Can be slightly more involved to configure initially due to modularity, but once set up, it's very quick for drivers to dock/undock.

**Specific RAM Mount Components to Consider:**

*   **RAM-B-166U (Suction Cup Base):** For non-permanent, strong windshield/dash mounting.
*   **RAM-B-309-1U (Clutch/Brake Reservoir Base):** If the vehicle has an accessible clutch/brake reservoir, this is incredibly sturdy.
*   **RAM-HOL-UN7BU (X-Grip Universal Phone Holder):** The most popular and versatile phone holder.
*   **RAM-HOL-PD3U (Quick-Grip Universal Phone Holder):** Excellent for one-handed operation.
*   **RAM-B-201U (Standard Double Socket Arm):** Connects the base to the holder.

### 2. iOttie Easy One Touch 5 Dash & Windshield Mount - **Best for Quick-Release Security & Ease of Use**

**Why it's #2:** While not "locking" in the traditional sense, the iOttie Easy One Touch series offers an incredibly secure grip on the phone and a very strong suction cup, making it highly resistant to accidental dislodgement. Its one-touch mechanism is ideal for shared vehicles where speed and simplicity are key.

*   **Commercial Use Cases:** Excellent for food delivery, ride-sharing, and light package delivery where drivers frequently enter/exit the vehicle and need quick phone access.
*   **Durability:** Very good. The suction cup is known for its longevity and strong hold. The plastic components are robust for daily use, though not as indestructible as RAM Mounts.
*   **Installation Style:** Suction cup for windshield or dashboard (comes with an adhesive dash pad for textured surfaces). Very quick and easy to install and remove.
*   **Tradeoffs:**
    *   **"Locking" Mechanism:** No physical key lock. The "locking" refers to the incredibly strong grip on the phone and the suction cup's hold. A determined thief could still pry the phone out or remove the mount.
    *   **Vibration Resistance:** Excellent for most road conditions, but extreme off-roading or very rough roads might challenge its limits compared to a drill-mounted RAM.
    *   **Dash Pad:** While useful, the adhesive dash pad is a one-time application and can leave residue if removed.
