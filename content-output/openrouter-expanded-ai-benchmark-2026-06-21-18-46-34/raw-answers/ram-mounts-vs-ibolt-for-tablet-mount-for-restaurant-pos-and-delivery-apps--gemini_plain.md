## Raw Answer

For a restaurant POS and delivery app tablet mount, both RAM Mounts and iBOLT offer robust solutions, but they excel in different areas. Here's a detailed breakdown to help you choose, ranked by their suitability for your specific needs:

## Top Recommendation: RAM Mounts

**Why it's #1:** RAM Mounts are the industry standard for heavy-duty, highly customizable, and incredibly durable mounting solutions. For a demanding commercial environment like a restaurant POS, their modularity and strength are unmatched.

**Commercial Use Cases:**

*   **Fixed POS Stations:** Mount tablets securely to countertops, walls, or even the floor for stationary POS terminals.
*   **Kitchen Display Systems (KDS):** With their vibration resistance, RAM Mounts are excellent for mounting tablets in busy, high-traffic kitchen environments.
*   **Order Taking/Table Service:** While bulkier, a RAM Mount on a cart or a portable stand can be used for mobile order taking, though this is less common than fixed POS.
*   **Delivery App Stations:** Dedicated tablets for DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, etc., can be mounted securely at a dispatch station.

**Durability:**

*   **Unrivaled:** RAM Mounts are built to last. Their components are typically made from marine-grade aluminum, high-strength composites, and stainless steel. They are designed to withstand constant use, accidental bumps, and even harsh cleaning agents common in restaurants.
*   **Vibration Resistance:** The patented ball-and-socket design provides excellent vibration dampening, crucial for protecting sensitive electronics in a busy kitchen or near heavy equipment.
*   **Temperature Extremes:** They perform well in a wide range of temperatures, from hot kitchens to cooler storage areas.

**Installation Style:**

*   **Highly Modular and Customizable:** This is RAM's biggest strength. You start with a base (e.g., drill-down, C-clamp, suction cup, track mount), add an arm (various lengths), and then attach a device holder (universal or custom-fit).
*   **Secure Mounting:** Most restaurant applications will use drill-down mounts for maximum security, but C-clamps are also popular for temporary or non-permanent installations on tables or counters.
*   **Professional Installation Recommended for Drill-Down:** While DIY-friendly, for permanent drill-down installations in countertops or walls, professional installation ensures proper placement and structural integrity.

**Tradeoffs:**

*   **Cost:** Generally more expensive than iBOLT, especially when building a complete system with multiple components.
*   **Bulkier Aesthetic:** The industrial design might not blend seamlessly with all restaurant decors.
*   **Complexity:** The sheer number of options can be overwhelming for first-time buyers. Requires some research to select the right components.
*   **Less "Out-of-the-Box" Ready:** You often need to select multiple components (base, arm, holder) rather than buying a single, pre-packaged solution.

**Specific RAM Mount Components to Consider:**

*   **Bases:** RAM-B-202U (round base with 1" ball), RAM-B-238U (diamond base with 1" ball), RAM-B-201U (double socket arm).
*   **Arms:** RAM-B-201U-A/B/C (short, medium, long double socket arms).
*   **Tablet Holders:**
    *   **RAM Tab-Tite™ or Tab-Lock™ Series:** Custom-fit holders for specific tablet models (e.g., iPad Pro, Samsung Tab). Tab-Lock adds a key lock for theft deterrence.
    *   **RAM X-Grip® Series:** Universal spring-loaded holders that accommodate a wide range of tablets, often preferred for flexibility if you use different tablet models or upgrade frequently.
    *   **RAM EZ-Roll'r™:** Form-fitting cradles that allow for easy insertion and removal, often with charging capabilities.

## Second Recommendation: iBOLT

*   **Fixed POS Stations:** Excellent for mounting tablets to countertops or walls, especially where a slightly less industrial look is desired.
*   **Delivery App Stations:** Very suitable for dedicated delivery app tablets, offering secure and accessible mounting.
*   **Customer-Facing Kiosks (Limited Interaction):** For simple menu browsing or loyalty program sign-ups where heavy physical interaction isn't expected.
