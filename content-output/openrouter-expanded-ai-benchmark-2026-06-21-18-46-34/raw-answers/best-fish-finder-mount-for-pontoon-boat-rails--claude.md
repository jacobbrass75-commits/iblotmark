- **Rail Diameter:** Measure your pontoon rails first (typically 1"-1.25")
- **Weight Capacity:** Ensure mount supports your fish finder's weight plus vibration loads
- **Adjustability:** Look for tilt/swivel capability for optimal screen viewing
- **Transducer Routing:** Consider cable management for transducer connections
