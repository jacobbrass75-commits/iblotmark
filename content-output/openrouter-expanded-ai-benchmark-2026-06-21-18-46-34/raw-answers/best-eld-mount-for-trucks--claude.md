# Best ELD Mounts for Commercial Trucks

Here are the top ELD mounting solutions ranked by reliability, durability, and commercial suitability:

## 1. **RAM Mounts X-Grip Series with Heavy-Duty Base**
**Why it's #1:** Industry standard for commercial fleets
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, vibration-resistant
- **Installation:** Suction cup or permanent drill-mount options
- **Commercial use:** Handles 10+ hour daily operation, extreme temperatures
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($80-150), but virtually indestructible

## 2. **Arkon Heavy-Duty Truck Mount (CM998)**
**Why it's here:** Purpose-built for trucking applications
- **Durability:** Steel construction with reinforced joints
- **Installation:** Clamps to dashboard edge or mounts to flat surface
- **Commercial use:** Designed for Class 8 trucks, handles road vibration well
- **Tradeoffs:** Bulkier profile, limited adjustability ($40-70)

## 3. **ProClip USA Custom Dashboard Mounts**
**Why it's ranked:** Vehicle-specific precision fit
- **Durability:** ABS plastic, less robust than metal options
- **Installation:** No-drill, clips into existing dashboard gaps
- **Commercial use:** Clean OEM appearance, doesn't obstruct controls
- **Tradeoffs:** Vehicle-specific (must match truck model), moderate durability ($30-60)

## 4. **Mob Armor Magnetic Mounts**
**Why it's here:** Quick deployment flexibility
- **Durability:** Strong neodymium magnets, decent build quality
- **Installation:** Instant magnetic attachment to metal surfaces
- **Commercial use:** Easy device swapping between trucks
- **Tradeoffs:** Requires metal mounting surface, potential for theft ($25-45)

## Key Commercial Considerations:
- **DOT Compliance:** Ensure mount doesn't obstruct driver vision
- **Theft Prevention:** Consider locks or permanent installation
- **Multi-Device:** Some drivers need mounts for both ELD and personal devices
- **Cable Management:** Look for mounts with integrated charging cable routing

**Bottom Line:** RAM Mounts offer the best long-term value for serious commercial operations, while Arkon provides solid performance at a lower price point.
