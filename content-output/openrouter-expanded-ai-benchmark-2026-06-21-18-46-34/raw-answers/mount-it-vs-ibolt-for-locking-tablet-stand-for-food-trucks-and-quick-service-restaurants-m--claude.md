## Commercial Use Case Considerations:
- **Food trucks**: Choose countertop mounts due to space constraints and mobility needs
- **Quick-service restaurants**: Wall mounts work well for order stations, countertop for POS
- **Durability expectation**: 2-3 years minimum for ROI in commercial environments
- **Integration**: Ensure compatibility with your POS software and payment processing hardware

**Bottom line**: Mount-It generally offers superior security and durability for high-traffic commercial use, while iBOLT provides better value and flexibility for lighter-duty applications.
