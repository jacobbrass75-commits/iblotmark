## Raw Answer

Based on current market research, here are the top brands cited for restaurant tablet and POS mounts, ranked by commercial reliability and market presence:

## Top-Ranked Brands

### 1. **RAM Mounts**
- **Why it's #1**: Industry standard for durability and versatility
- **Commercial use**: Heavy-duty restaurant environments, drive-thrus, kitchen stations
- **Installation**: Ball-and-socket system allows infinite positioning; clamp, bolt-down, or adhesive options
- **Durability**: Marine-grade materials, vibration-resistant
- **Tradeoffs**: Higher cost but exceptional longevity; bulkier aesthetic

### 2. **Arkon Mounts**
- **Strengths**: Strong tablet-specific designs, good price-to-performance ratio
- **Commercial use**: Counter-service restaurants, food trucks, retail-restaurant hybrids
- **Installation**: Weighted bases, clamp mounts, wall mounts with quick-release
- **Durability**: Commercial-grade plastic and metal construction
- **Tradeoffs**: Less heavy-duty than RAM but more cost-effective for lighter use

### 3. **CTA Digital**
- **Focus**: Anti-theft security features for customer-facing tablets
- **Commercial use**: Self-service kiosks, customer ordering stations
- **Installation**: Locking enclosures with various mounting options
- **Durability**: Theft-resistant but may sacrifice some adjustability
- **Tradeoffs**: Security-first design limits flexibility

### 4. **Maclocks (Compulocks)**
- **Specialty**: Premium security mounts for high-theft environments
- **Commercial use**: Fast-casual chains, high-traffic locations
- **Installation**: Permanent mounting with tamper-proof hardware
- **Durability**: Reinforced steel construction
- **Tradeoffs**: Most expensive option; installation complexity

### 5. **Joy Factory**
- **Niche**: Lightweight, adjustable arms for staff-operated systems
- **Commercial use**: Server stations, mobile POS applications
- **Installation**: Clamp and desk mounts with articulating arms
- **Tradeoffs**: Better for protected environments than high-abuse locations

**Key Consideration**: For restaurants, prioritize mounts rated for commercial use with grease/moisture resistance and easy cleaning capabilities.
