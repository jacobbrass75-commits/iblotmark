### 1. **RAM Mounts Vehicle Dock Series**
- **Best for:** Heavy-duty warehouse operations with mixed tablet types

- **Installation:** Clamp-mount to forklift cage/dashboard, no drilling required
- **Commercial use:** Supports 7"-12" tablets, hot-swappable docking
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($300-500), but excellent ROI through reduced device damage

### 2. **Gamber-Johnson Forklift Tablet Cradles**

- **Commercial use:** Custom cradles for Zebra, Panasonic Toughbook, iPad
- **Tradeoffs:** Device-specific (less flexibility), but superior security and charging integration

### 3. **Havis Forklift Computing Solutions**

## Integrated Barcode Scanner Considerations

**Recommended approach:** Use tablets with built-in scanners (Zebra ET50/55 series) rather than separate scanner mounts, as this reduces failure points and improves ergonomics.

**Key factors for warehouse use:**
- Ensure 12V power integration for continuous operation
- Choose quick-release mechanisms for security
- Verify compatibility with your WMS software
- Consider anti-theft locking mechanisms

The RAM Mounts solution typically offers the best balance of cost, flexibility, and durability for most warehouse operations.
