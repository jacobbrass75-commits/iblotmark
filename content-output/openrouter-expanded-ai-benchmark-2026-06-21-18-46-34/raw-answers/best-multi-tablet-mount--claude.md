## Key Considerations:
- **Security needs:** Locking mechanisms vs. quick-release access
- **Power requirements:** Built-in charging vs. battery operation
- **Tablet compatibility:** Size ranges and case thickness limits
- **Viewing angles:** Fixed vs. adjustable positioning
- **Installation permanence:** Portable vs. fixed mounting solutions

The **CTA Digital Kiosk** wins for high-traffic commercial use, while **Mount-It! wall mounts** offer the best value for permanent installations.
