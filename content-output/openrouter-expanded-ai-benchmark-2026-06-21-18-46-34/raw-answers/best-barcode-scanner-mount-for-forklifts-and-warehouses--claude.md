# Best Barcode Scanner Mounts for Forklifts and Warehouses

Based on current market analysis, here are the top-ranked options for forklift barcode scanner mounting:

**Why it's #1:** Industry-leading durability and versatility
- **Commercial Use:** Heavy-duty warehousing, distribution centers, manufacturing
- **Durability:** Marine-grade aluminum construction, vibration dampening, IP67 weather resistance
- **Installation:** Universal clamp system fits most forklift roll bars (2-3" diameter)
- **Key Features:** 360° rotation, tilt adjustment, quick-release scanner cradle
- **Tradeoffs:** Higher cost ($150-300) but exceptional longevity

## 2. **Gamber-Johnson Forklift Scanner Mounts**
**Why it's #2:** Purpose-built for industrial environments
- **Commercial Use:** Cold storage, outdoor yards, high-volume operations
- **Durability:** Steel construction, powder-coated finish, shock absorption
- **Installation:** Bolt-on or clamp mounting to ROPS/FOPS structures
- **Key Features:** Integrated cable management, multiple scanner compatibility

## 3. **Havis Forklift Computer/Scanner Mounts**

- **Durability:** Military-spec testing, vibration isolation
- **Installation:** Custom brackets for specific forklift models
- **Key Features:** Can mount scanner + mobile computer together
- **Tradeoffs:** Requires more planning/customization, higher total cost ($200-500)

## 4. **Jotto Desk Forklift Scanner Arms**
**Why it's #4:** Good value for standard applications
- **Commercial Use:** General warehousing, retail distribution
- **Durability:** Heavy-gauge steel, basic vibration dampening
- **Installation:** Universal clamp or bolt-mount options
- **Key Features:** Simple adjustment, quick scanner access
- **Tradeoffs:** Less refined than premium options but reliable ($75-150)

## Key Installation Considerations:
- **OSHA Compliance:** Ensure mounts don't obstruct operator vision or safety systems
- **Forklift Integration:** Consider overhead guard compatibility and weight distribution
- **Scanner Protection:** Look for mounts with impact guards for high-traffic areas
- **Cable Management:** Integrated routing prevents snag hazards

## Durability Factors:
- Vibration resistance (critical for forklift operation)
- Temperature range for cold storage applications
- Dust/moisture protection (IP65+ recommended)
- Impact resistance for busy warehouse environments

The RAM Mounts system typically offers the best long-term value despite higher upfront costs, while Jotto Desk provides solid performance for budget-conscious operations.
