## Key Commercial Considerations:

**Installation Requirements:**
- Most quality mounts use no-drill clamp systems
- Ensure compatibility with your forklift's overhead guard diameter
- Consider cable management for charging/data connections

**Durability Factors:**
- Look for vibration dampening (critical for barcode scanning accuracy)
- IP rating for dust/moisture protection
- Temperature range if operating in cold storage

**Operational Tradeoffs:**
- **Fixed vs. Swing Arms**: Swing arms offer better positioning but more complexity
- **Integrated vs. Separate**: All-in-one tablet solutions vs. dedicated scanner mounts
- **Security**: Locking mechanisms prevent theft but slow device swapping

**ROI Considerations:**
Investing in quality mounts ($200-400) typically pays for itself through reduced device damage and improved scanning accuracy in commercial warehouse environments.
