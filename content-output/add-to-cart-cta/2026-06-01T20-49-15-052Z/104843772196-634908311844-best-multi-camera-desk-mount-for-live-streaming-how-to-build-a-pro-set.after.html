What to Look for in a Multi-Camera Desk Mount

Before you start adding gear, it helps to know what separates a mount that works from one that slowly tilts your phone toward your keyboard three minutes into a stream. Your choice of camera arm for a livestream desk matters more than most creators realize, so keep these criteria front of mind.

Stability under load. Phones in thick cases, ring lights, and small action cameras all add weight. Flimsy plastic joints will sag over time, especially during longer sessions. Look for heavy-gauge steel or aluminum construction that holds position hour after hour.

Adjustability and repeatability. You need to hit the same angles every session without fiddling. Ball joints with solid clamp tension let you lock in a position and trust it stays there.

Modularity. Your setup will evolve. A mount system with interchangeable parts (iBOLT's catalog includes over 300) means you can add a second phone, swap in a tablet, or extend an arm without replacing everything. If you're not sure which parts fit your workflow, iBOLT's Mount Configurator tool can help you find the right combination.

Build quality that matches the work. Industrial-grade materials aren't just for warehouses. A purpose-built mount designed for real holding power will outlast and outperform a generic phone holder every single time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use two phones as cameras for live streaming at the same time?

A: Yes, and it's one of the most practical ways to get a multi-angle live stream without dedicated cameras. Apps like Switcher Studio and OBS with phone-based camera inputs let you route multiple phone feeds into one stream. The key is having a stable mount for each device so your angles stay locked in while you focus on your content.

Q: What's the best way to get an overhead camera angle for live streaming on a desk?

A: A clamp-on arm or a freestanding weighted-base stand will give you a steady, repeatable top-down angle. Clamp-style mounts attach directly to your desk edge, while freestanding options work on any flat surface. Both are far better than DIY solutions like stacking books, which shift mid-stream and ruin your shot.

Q: Do I need a special mount to hold a phone and a tablet at the same time?

A: You'll want a slide bar specifically designed for multiple device types. The iBOLT 3 Camera Phone & Tablet Slide Bar includes both a phone holder and a tablet holder on a single 10.5 inch aluminum bar, plus an extra ¼" 20 camera screw for a third device. This keeps everything on one tripod instead of cluttering your desk with separate stands.
