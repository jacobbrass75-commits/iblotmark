Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use my car tablet mount in my commercial truck for ELD compliance?

A: Consumer-grade mounts typically won't withstand the constant vibration, temperature extremes, and daily abuse that commercial trucks experience. FMCSA regulations also require specific visibility and accessibility standards that many car mounts can't meet.

Q: What's the difference between AMPS mounting patterns and why does it matter for ELD mounts?

A: AMPS (Amphenol Military Performance Specification) refers to standardized hole patterns that ensure compatibility between mounting bases and device holders. The 4-hole AMPS pattern (75mm x 75mm spacing) is industry standard, meaning you can mix and match components from different manufacturers while maintaining secure connections.

Q: Do I need a locking mount for my ELD tablet?

A: Locking mounts provide crucial theft protection for expensive ELD devices, especially for drivers who park overnight at truck stops or unsecured locations. Even if you remove your tablet nightly, a locking system prevents opportunistic theft during fuel stops or loading delays.

Q: How do I know if my ELD mount meets DOT visibility requirements?

A: FMCSA regulations require ELD displays to be within your normal driving position and readily visible without obstructing your view. The device should be reachable without unbuckling your seatbelt and positioned so DOT officers can easily view it during inspections.

Q: Can I mount both my ELD tablet and smartphone using the same mounting system?

A: Yes, modular mounting systems like iBOLT's allow you to position multiple devices using compatible bases and arms. The key is choosing complementary mounting positions that keep your ELD tablet as the primary display while providing convenient access to your phone for backup navigation or communication.

Q: What mounting method works best for drivers who switch between different trucks?

A: Suction cup mounts offer the best portability for drivers who don't have dedicated trucks. Look for triple suction designs that provide stability while allowing quick setup and removal. Magnetic mounts work well if trucks have suitable steel surfaces, but avoid single suction cup or weak magnetic solutions that compromise security.
