Buyer’s Guide: How to Select the Best Fish Finder Mount for Your Small Boat

Selecting the best fish finder mount for a small boat involves considering a few key factors:

  1. Material and Durability

Small boats face saltwater, sun exposure, and vibration. Look for mounts made from heavy-gauge steel, powder-coated aluminum, or corrosion-resistant composites. These materials ensure your mount holds up season after season.

  1. Compatibility

Check your fish finder’s mounting requirements and your boat’s available mounting points. iBOLT’s modular system supports common industry ball sizes (25mm, 38mm, 1.5 inch) and AMPS patterns, allowing easy integration with your existing gear.

  1. Installation Style

Decide if you want a permanent mount or a portable clamp-on solution. Drill-base mounts provide stability but require installation effort. Clamp mounts offer flexibility and quick removal, ideal for jon boats or kayaks without permanent mounting surfaces.

  1. Adjustability and Space

Consider mounts with adjustable arms or dual-ball designs for better positioning in tight spaces. This helps you keep your fish finder visible and accessible without cluttering your small boat.
