Common Setup Mistakes To Avoid

The most common mistake is choosing by mount style alone. A suction base, wedge base, drill base, locking cradle, or rail mount can all be the right answer in the right setting. The better question is how the device gets used during a normal workday: who touches it, how often it moves, whether it needs charging, and whether it stays in a shared vehicle or public-facing counter area.

Another mistake is treating the holder and the base as separate decisions. For magnetic vs clamp phone mounts for delivery work, the base controls stability and placement, while the holder controls device security and daily usability. If either side is wrong, the whole setup feels wrong even if the product looks close on paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are magnetic mounts good for delivery drivers?

They can work for lighter personal delivery use, but they are less secure than clamp or locking mounts during rough route work.

Are clamp phone mounts better than magnetic mounts?

For delivery work, clamp mounts are usually better because they physically hold the phone. They are less dependent on case thickness, magnet strength, or phone weight.

When should I use a locking phone mount?

Use a locking mount for shared vehicles, company phones, or any route setup where the phone should stay secured in the vehicle.

Do clamp mounts slow drivers down?

A good cradle should be fast enough for daily use while adding more security than a magnetic puck.
