Types of Forklift Tablet Mounting Solutions

Understanding the different mounting approaches helps you choose the right solution for each forklift model and application in your warehouse. Three primary categories each offer distinct advantages depending on your operational needs and equipment constraints.

Pillar mounts represent the most popular choice for forklift tablet mounting. These systems clamp directly to the vertical support pillars of your forklift's overhead guard, positioning tablets at optimal viewing angles while keeping them secure during operation. Need a reliable workhorse mount? The iBOLT TabDock Bizmount Pillar Mount features a heavy-duty 25mm ball joint system that withstands constant vibration while allowing 360-degree positioning adjustments. For operations requiring theft protection, the iBOLT Dock'n Lock Bizmount Forklift Locking Tablet Mount adds security with integrated lock mechanisms.

Cage mounts attach to the overhead guard framework itself, distributing mounting stress across multiple connection points. When pillar space is limited by existing forklift accessories like lights or mirrors, or when you're mounting larger tablets, cage mounting provides a stable alternative.

Magnetic solutions offer the ultimate in flexibility and speed of installation. Looking for maximum versatility?
