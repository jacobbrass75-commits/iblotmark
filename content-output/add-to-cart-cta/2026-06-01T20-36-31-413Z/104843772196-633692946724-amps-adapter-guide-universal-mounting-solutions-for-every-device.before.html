What is an AMPS Adapter and Why Do You Need One?

An AMPS adapter is a connector plate that bridges the gap between different mounting systems using the industry-standard AMPS pattern. AMPS (Advanced Mobile Phone System) refers to the universal 4-hole mounting pattern with holes spaced 30.17mm x 38.10mm apart. Think of it as the "universal language" that allows different devices and mounts to communicate with each other.

The beauty of the AMPS pattern lies in its versatility. Whether you're mounting tablets in commercial vehicles, setting up monitors in warehouses, or installing devices in marine environments, AMPS adapters ensure compatibility across brands and industries. Instead of buying entirely new mounting systems, you can simply add an adapter to make your existing equipment work together.

Understanding AMPS Mount Compatibility

When you're shopping for mounting solutions, you'll encounter the term "AMPS" constantly, but what does it actually mean for your setup? AMPS (Amphenol Military Specification) refers to a standardized hole pattern that creates universal compatibility across thousands of devices and mounting systems. This 30.17mm x 38.10mm spacing (roughly 1.19" x 1.5") ensures that an AMPS-compatible tablet holder will work with any AMPS mount base, regardless of manufacturer.

Think of AMPS compatibility as the USB-C of the mounting world. Once you understand this standard, building custom mounting solutions becomes remarkably straightforward. The four-hole pattern appears on everything from GPS devices and tablets to barcode scanners and marine electronics.
