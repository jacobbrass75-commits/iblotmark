What Garmin GPS systems use an AMPS Pattern Screw mount?

iBOLT has many AMPS pattern mounts in different sizes, shapes, and material configurations.  The AMPS pattern is a 4-Hole rectangular pattern spaced at 30mm by 38mm. This industry-standard pattern is widely used in the mounting industry for vehicle-specific brackets, attaching devices, and more. iBOLT offers a 2-Hole diamond adapter that matched the 4-hole AMPS pattern diagonally.
