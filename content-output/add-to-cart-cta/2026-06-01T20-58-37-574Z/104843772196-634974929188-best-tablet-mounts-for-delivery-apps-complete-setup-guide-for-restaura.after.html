Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many tablets do I need for a restaurant delivery operation?

A: Most restaurants start with one tablet and add more as they join additional delivery platforms. A busy restaurant typically needs separate tablets for DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub to avoid constantly switching between apps during peak hours.

Q: What's the difference between consumer tablet stands and restaurant-grade mounts?

A: Restaurant-grade mounts feature heavy-gauge steel construction, powder coating to resist grease and moisture, and weighted or drill-down bases that stay secure in busy kitchen environments. Consumer stands typically can't handle the constant use and vibration of commercial kitchens.

Q: Can I mount tablets on the wall to save counter space?

A: Yes, wall-mounted tablet holders are excellent for saving counter space while keeping delivery platforms visible. The iBOLT wall mount series accommodates multiple tablets in a vertical configuration that maximizes space efficiency.

Q: Do I need locking tablet mounts for my restaurant?

A: Locking mounts are recommended for restaurants where tablets might be accessible to customers or in high-traffic areas. They prevent theft while allowing authorized staff quick access to manage orders and confirmations.

Q: How do I position tablets for optimal order management workflow?

A: Position tablets at eye level near prep stations where kitchen staff can easily see incoming orders without interrupting their cooking workflow. Avoid placing mounts where they'll interfere with food preparation or create reach-across-heat situations.

Q: Are iBOLT tablet mounts compatible with all delivery app tablets?

A: iBOLT mounts accommodate standard tablet sizes used by major delivery platforms. The adjustable holders work with iPads and Android tablets commonly provided by DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub for restaurant partners.
