Not every vehicle allows drilling into the dashboard. Maybe it's a leased truck. Maybe fleet policy prohibits permanent modifications. Fits More Cup Holders Than You'd Expect

The base expands from 2.5" to 3.5" in diameter, which covers the range from standard passenger car cup holders up to the oversized ones in semi-truck consoles and Sprinter vans. That adjustability matters when you're deploying across a mixed fleet — the same mount works in a sedan, a pickup, and a box truck without stocking different SKUs.

Real Stability, Not Just a Suggestion

Four strong contact points grip the tablet firmly, and the mount uses dual 1-inch (25mm/B size) ball joints on a 3.75-inch pedestal for full 360-degree rotation and tilt. You can swing the tablet from portrait to landscape, adjust the viewing angle for tall or short drivers, and reposition it for different tasks — all without tools.

The horizontal insertion design means you slide the tablet in from the side, and the four-point contact holds it without slipping. The ball joints lock tight enough to hold position through potholes and highway driving, but adjust smoothly when you want to change the angle.
