Understanding AMPS Pattern Mount Systems: The Universal Standard

When building a modular tablet mount, understanding AMPS patterns is crucial to creating a truly versatile system. AMPS (Amphenol Military Specification) patterns are the standardized hole configurations that enable different mounting components to work together across brands and manufacturers.

The most common AMPS configuration is the 4-hole rectangular pattern measuring 30mm x 38mm (about 1.18" x 1.5"). This standard ensures that your tablet holder, mounting arms, and base components can interchange freely, giving you maximum flexibility in your setup. Whether you're mounting an iPad in a delivery truck or a Samsung tablet on a forklift, AMPS compatibility means your investment in mounting hardware pays dividends across different applications.
