Base Selection for Different Booth Configurations

Drill-base mounts work when you can modify the booth counter or table surface. These provide the strongest connection and best theft deterrence, but require planning during booth design. Most exhibit halls allow drilling into custom booth furniture, but verify this with your venue before the event.

Clamp-style bases attach to existing table edges or booth rails without permanent modification. This approach works well for rented booth space or when the same mount needs to work across multiple events with different table configurations.

Weighted bases offer portability without requiring attachment to the booth structure. These work best for tablets that need to move during the event or when the mounting surface can't support drilling or clamping.

Security and Device Management Considerations

Locking mechanisms prevent casual device removal but shouldn't make legitimate access difficult for staff. iBOLT's locking systems use keys rather than complex combinations, which matters when multiple team members need device access during busy show periods.

Cable management becomes critical in high-traffic areas. Charging cables that cross walkways create trip hazards and look unprofessional. Route power and data cables through the mount structure or under the booth counter before finalizing the installation.

Device rotation and tilt adjustment help accommodate users of different heights, which matters more in public settings than in controlled office environments. Verify that the mount allows screen positioning changes without tools, since booth staff may need to adjust viewing angles throughout the day.

Installation Planning and Event Logistics

Measure your devices with their protective cases installed. Trade show tablets typically need more protection than office devices, and thicker cases affect holder compatibility. If you're using barcode scanners, card readers, or other accessories, verify that these don't interfere with the mounting hardware.

Plan for setup and breakdown time. Mounts that require extensive assembly slow down booth installation when you're working under tight show schedules. iBOLT's modular approach lets you pre-assemble holder and arm combinations, then attach them to bases that are already installed on booth furniture.

Consider standardization across multiple events. Using the same mounting system for all your trade show tablets simplifies inventory management and reduces the chance of arriving at a show with incompatible parts.

Can these mounts work with tablets in protective cases?

Yes, but verify case compatibility before ordering. Measure your device with its case installed and check that ports remain accessible. Heavy-duty cases may require larger holders or different retention mechanisms.

How do I prevent cables from becoming trip hazards?

Route charging and data cables through the mount structure when possible, or secure them under booth counters with cable management clips. Avoid running cables across walkways or areas where visitors congregate.

What's the difference between drill-base and clamp-base mounts?

Drill-base mounts attach permanently to booth furniture and provide maximum security and stability. Clamp-base mounts attach to table edges without modification and work better for rented booth space or multi-event use.
