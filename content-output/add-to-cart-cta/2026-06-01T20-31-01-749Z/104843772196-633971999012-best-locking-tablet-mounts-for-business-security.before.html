Tablets in a commercial setting have a way of disappearing. It's not always theft — sometimes they get moved between vehicles, borrowed by another crew, or left somewhere during a shift change. But the result is the same: downtime while someone tracks down the device, and real cost when one needs to be replaced entirely.

A locking tablet mount solves the problem at the source. Once installed, the tablet stays put — secured with keys and tamper-resistant hardware that keeps things where they belong. Here are three locking mount options from iBOLT, each built for a different combination of budget, material, and use case.

Budget-Friendly Security: Dock'n Lock Bizmount AMPS Drill Base

The iBOLT Dock'n Lock Bizmount AMPS Locking Drill Base Mount ($49.95) delivers real security at a price that makes sense when you're outfitting multiple vehicles or workstations. It fits tablets from 7" to 11" and uses a tamper-resistant locking system with secure keys and tamper-proof hex bolts that prevent unauthorized removal.

The build uses heavy-duty industrial composite — not as heavy as metal, but plenty durable for commercial use and lighter on the mounting surface. Dual 1-inch (25mm) ball joints give you full 360-degree rotation and tilt, so operators can adjust the viewing angle without tools. The 3.75-inch composite pedestal keeps the tablet elevated and easy to reach.

What's in the box: tablet holder, composite arm, AMPS plates, secure keys, tamper-resistant hardware, and installation screws. Everything you need for a clean install.

Best for: Fleet vehicles, ELD compliance setups, delivery vans, and any operation where you need locking security across multiple units without a large per-unit investment.

The iBOLT Dock'n Lock IncrediBOLT 360 AMPS Locking Tablet Mount ($82.95) steps up in build quality while keeping the same core security features. It supports 7" to 10" tablets and combines key-lock protection with full 360-degree rotational control.

The IncrediBOLT design brings a sturdier construction that's built for rougher environments — think semi-truck cabs, police vehicles, agricultural equipment, and construction sites where the mount takes daily abuse from vibration and weather. This is the go-to choice for trucking companies running ELD tablets. The lock keeps the device secure during driver changes, and the 360-degree adjustment means both tall and short drivers can position the screen where they need it without a wrench.

When you need maximum durability and the most secure hold possible, the iBOLT LockPro Metal Locking Tablet Drill Base Mount ($99.95) is the top of the line. The LockPro series uses durable aluminum construction with a composite pedestal shaft — it's built to outlast the vehicles it's mounted in.

Like the other Dock'n Lock mounts, it uses tamper-resistant hardware with secure keys to prevent theft. The dual 1-inch (25mm) ball joints deliver smooth 360-degree rotation and tilt. It fits tablets from 7" to 11" and is compatible with all industry-standard AMPS mounting arms.
