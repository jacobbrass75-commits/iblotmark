Installation Best Practices

Proper installation makes all the difference between a mount that performs flawlessly and one that fails when you need it most. Start with surface preparation—clean mounting surfaces thoroughly with isopropyl alcohol to remove oils, dust, and residue that can compromise adhesion.

For drill-base installations, use the included templates to ensure accurate hole placement and always pre-drill pilot holes to prevent material cracking. When working with ball-and-socket connections, apply firm pressure while tightening to achieve optimal grip without over-torquing. The rubber-coated balls in iBolt systems are designed to provide secure hold while protecting your device from vibration.

For permanent installations requiring extra security, consider the Security Hardware for 25mm Bizmount Set of 3 Bolts, 1 Hex Key. This upgrade replaces removable knobs with tamper-resistant bolts, perfect for commercial vehicles and public-facing installations where theft prevention is crucial.
