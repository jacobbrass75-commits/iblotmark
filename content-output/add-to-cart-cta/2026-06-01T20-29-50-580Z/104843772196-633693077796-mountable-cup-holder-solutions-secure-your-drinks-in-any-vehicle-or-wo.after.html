Mounting Options: Clamps, Adhesive, and Permanent Installation

Your mounting method determines both installation ease and long-term reliability, with each approach serving different scenarios from temporary setups to permanent installations.

C-Clamp Mounting offers maximum versatility for desks, tables, and vehicle consoles. This approach works perfectly when you need portability or can't drill permanent holes. The clamping mechanism accommodates various surface thicknesses while providing secure attachment that can be repositioned as needed.

VHB Adhesive Mounting provides a semi-permanent solution for smooth surfaces like dashboards, desks, or equipment panels. Industrial-grade VHB (Very High Bond) tape creates strong, lasting holds without drilling. This method excels on clean, flat surfaces where you want the cup holder to stay put but may eventually need removal for cleaning or reconfiguration.

Drill-Base Installation delivers maximum security for permanent setups in fleet vehicles, industrial equipment, or workstations where the cup holder becomes a permanent fixture. While requiring more installation effort, this approach provides unmatched stability for heavy-duty applications.

Consider your specific needs carefully: clamps offer flexibility for changing requirements, adhesive provides clean installations on smooth surfaces, and drill-base mounting ensures maximum security when stability matters most. Each method accommodates different cup sizes and mounting scenarios while maintaining the beverage security your application demands.
