If you've ever looked into mounting a tablet in a work truck or on a desk and seen the term "AMPS pattern" everywhere, here's what it means in practice: AMPS is a standardized 4-hole mounting pattern (30mm x 38mm spacing) used across the commercial mounting industry. It's the same pattern used by RAM, iBOLT, and dozens of other manufacturers — which means any AMPS-compatible mount works with any AMPS-compatible base, arm, or adapter.

That matters because it means you're not locked into one brand. You can mix and match components, swap bases, add arms, and build out a mounting setup that fits your exact vehicle or workspace. iBOLT offers three AMPS drill base tablet mounts at different price points, each built for a slightly different use case. Here's how they compare.

Entry-Level: TabDock Bizmount AMPS ($29.95)

The iBOLT TabDock Bizmount AMPS (from $29.95) is the starting point for anyone who needs a solid drill base tablet mount without overspending. At under $30 for the standard TabDock and $34.95 for the TabDockXL, it's priced right for fleet managers outfitting multiple vehicles or shops setting up several workstations.

The mount uses a 3.75-inch composite BizMount arm with dual 1-inch (25mm) ball joints for 360-degree adjustment. The four-point contact system grips tablets from 7" to 11" — including devices in bulky protective cases, which is a detail that matters in commercial settings where ruggedized cases are standard.

What's in the box: TabDock tablet holder, 25mm-to-4-prong adapter, 3.75" composite BizMount arm, 25mm AMPS plate, and screw/nut pack. Everything for a clean drill base install on any flat surface — dashboards, desks, walls, or custom mounting surfaces.

Best for: Fleet vehicles needing ELD mounts, office workstations, multi-unit deployments where per-unit cost matters.

Mid-Range: TabDock FixedPro 360 ($43.25)

The TabDock FixedPro 360 (from $43.25 for TabDock, $48.95 for TabDockXL) steps up from the Bizmount with metal construction. That's not a cosmetic difference — in high-impact environments like construction vehicles, agricultural equipment, or police cruisers, the metal build resists the kind of wear that loosens composite mounts over time.

The "360" in the name refers to the multi-angle adjustment that lets you position the tablet exactly where you need it. For truckers meeting ELD mandate requirements, this mount has earned a 4.84-star rating across 94 reviews — the kind of track record that comes from thousands of miles of real-world use.

The FixedPro hits a sweet spot between the Bizmount's value and the IncrediBOLT's premium build. It's the right pick when you need metal durability but don't need the IncrediBOLT's extra features.

Best for: Semi-trucks, commercial fleets, delivery vans, agriculture, law enforcement, and healthcare vehicles.

Premium: TabDock IncrediBOLT 360 AMPS ($64.95)

The iBOLT TabDock IncrediBOLT 360 AMPS ($64.95 for TabDock, $69.95 for TabDockXL) is the heavy-duty option for demanding environments. The IncrediBOLT arm system delivers what iBOLT calls "unwavering stability" — and if you've used cheaper mounts that vibrate loose over time, you know exactly what problem that solves.

Built for high-mobility professionals — truckers, delivery drivers, towing operators, agricultural workers — this mount is designed to handle the vibration and impact that comes with driving all day on every type of road surface. The AMPS-pattern base connects to standard mounting points, and the IncrediBOLT arm provides a stronger clamping force that keeps the ball joints locked in position.

Like the other two mounts, it fits tablets from 7" to 11" and works with iPads, Samsung Galaxy Tabs, and Amazon Fire devices. The difference is in how it performs after six months of daily use — the IncrediBOLT design holds its position without the gradual loosening that affects simpler ball joint systems.

Best for: Long-haul trucking, towing, agricultural equipment, paramedic vehicles, and any professional setup where the mount gets heavy daily use.
