ELD tablets have become the main screen for logging, dispatch, routing, inspections, messaging, and fleet apps. The mount has to keep that screen visible without turning it into a loose device on the seat, dash, or cup holder. This collection brings together iBOLT tablet mounts for commercial trucks, vans, delivery vehicles, service fleets, and shared work vehicles.

Start with the vehicle and driver workflow. Seat rail mounts work well when you need a tablet near the center console without attaching to the windshield. AMPS drill-base mounts are better for fixed fleet installs, wall panels, consoles, and vehicle-specific brackets. Suction and cup holder mounts can help with temporary setups, personal vehicles, and mixed-use fleets. Locking tablet holders are useful when devices stay in the vehicle between shifts.

For fleet managers, standardizing on iBOLT's modular ball sizes and AMPS parts can make replacement easier across vehicle types. Drivers get a consistent tablet position, and installers get a repeatable mounting system for ELD, GPS, inspection, and communication devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best mount for an ELD tablet?

The best ELD mount depends on the vehicle. Seat rail, AMPS drill-base, suction, and locking mounts all work, but the tablet must be visible and should not block controls or sightlines.

Should fleet tablets use locking holders?

Locking holders are recommended when tablets stay in shared vehicles, are assigned to crews, or need extra protection from theft and accidental removal.

Do AMPS mounts work for commercial vehicles?

Yes. AMPS is a common pattern for vehicle brackets, adapter plates, and installed tablet setups in commercial vehicles.

Can one fleet use the same mounting system across different vehicles?

Often, yes. Modular iBOLT arms, bases, holders, and adapter plates help teams keep similar tablet setups across trucks, vans, and service vehicles.
