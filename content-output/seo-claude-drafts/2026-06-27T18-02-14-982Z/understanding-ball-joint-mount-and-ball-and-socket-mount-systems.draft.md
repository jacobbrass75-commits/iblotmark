## Why Ball Size Matters More Than Most Buyers Expect

A ball-and-socket mount works by pressing a spherical ball into a matching socket and tightening a clamp to lock the angle. The mechanism is simple and reliable, but only when the ball diameter matches the socket diameter. Use a 25mm ball in a 17mm socket and it will not seat. Use a 17mm ball in a 25mm socket and it will rattle loose under vibration.

Most mounting ecosystems are built around one ball size. Garmin GPS units use a 17mm ball. Many consumer phone mounts use a 17mm or 20mm ball. Heavy-duty vehicle and industrial arms typically use a 25mm (1-inch) ball, sometimes called a B-size ball. Larger industrial systems step up to a 38mm (1.5-inch) C-size ball. When you buy components from different ecosystems, you need an adapter to bridge the size difference.

**22mm ball:** Found in certain mounting arms and heads, particularly in systems that sit between the consumer 17mm world and the professional 25mm world. The iBOLT dual-ball adapters described below output a 22mm ball, making them a useful bridge point.

**25mm (1-inch) ball:** The standard for heavy-duty vehicle mounts, truck and fleet installations, marine electronics, and industrial arms. This size is sometimes called a B-size ball. ## Glossary: Ball Mount Terms You Will See on Product Pages

**Ball mount:** A mounting component with a spherical ball that seats into a matching socket to allow multi-directional adjustment.

**Socket:** The receiving end of a ball-and-socket system. It clamps around the ball to lock position.

**B-size ball:** Industry shorthand for a 25mm (1-inch) ball, common in professional and heavy-duty vehicle mounts.

**AMPS adapter:** A plate or bracket that converts an AMPS hole pattern (a four-hole grid used on many device cradles and holders) to a ball mount or other interface. AMPS adapters are often used to attach device-specific cradles to universal arms.

**Dual ball adapter:** An adapter that takes one ball input and outputs two balls, allowing two holders to attach to a single arm or allowing a size conversion with a redundant connection point.

**20mm ball mount accessories:** Components designed around the 20mm ball standard, including sockets, arms, and holders. Less common than 17mm or 25mm but present in certain consumer and semi-professional product lines.

 

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most common ball size for phone and GPS mounts?

The 17mm ball is the most common size in consumer phone and GPS mounts, including Garmin devices. The 25mm (1-inch) ball is the standard for heavy-duty vehicle and industrial arms.

### Can I use a 17mm holder on a 25mm arm?

Not directly. You need an adapter. The iBOLT 25mm to Dual 17mm Metal Ball Adapter converts a 25mm arm output to two 17mm balls so you can attach a 17mm-compatible holder.

### What does an AMPS adapter do?

An AMPS adapter connects a device cradle that has an AMPS hole pattern to a mounting arm or ball socket that does not natively accept AMPS plates. It is a common bridge between device-specific cradles and universal mounting arms.

### Is a 20mm ball the same as a 22mm ball?

No. They are close in size but not interchangeable. Always confirm the exact diameter before ordering a socket or adapter. Using a slightly undersized ball in a socket can cause slippage under vibration.

### How do I know which adapter I need?

Measure the ball on your arm and the socket on your holder. Match the arm-side ball to the adapter input and the holder-side socket to the adapter output.
