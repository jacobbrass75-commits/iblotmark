What Is an Electronic Logging Device?

An Electronic Logging Device (ELD) is a digital system that records driving time and other operational data for commercial motor vehicles. ELDs are commonly used to help carriers and drivers comply with Hours of Service (HOS) regulations.

These devices typically track:

  • Driving hours
  • Duty status changes
  • Vehicle movement
  • Engine operation data
  • Mileage information
  • Location information

Many ELD systems operate through dedicated hardware units, tablets, smartphones, or integrated fleet management platforms.

Why Proper ELD Placement Matters

Commercial drivers interact with ELD systems throughout the workday. They may use the device to:

  • Review duty status
  • Log operational changes
  • Access dispatch information
  • View route details
  • Monitor compliance records

Improper device placement can create challenges such as:

  • Reduced screen visibility
  • Difficulty accessing controls
  • Excessive device movement
  • Cab clutter
  • Increased risk of device damage

A dedicated ELD Mount helps address these concerns by providing a secure location for the device.

What Is an ELD Mount?

An ELD Mount is a mounting solution designed to hold an electronic logging device, tablet, or smartphone inside a commercial vehicle.

These mounts are engineered to keep devices securely positioned while allowing drivers to view and access information when necessary.

Depending on the fleet's technology platform, an Electronic Logging Device Mount may support:

  • Dedicated ELD hardware
  • Tablets
  • Smartphones
  • Mobile computers
  • Fleet management displays

Common Types of ELD Mounting Solutions

Dashboard Mounts

Dashboard mounts attach directly to the vehicle dashboard and position the device within the driver's field of view.

Benefits include:

  • Easy accessibility
  • Compact installation
  • Convenient screen visibility

Windshield Mounts

Windshield-mounted systems use suction or attachment mechanisms to secure the device to the vehicle windshield.

These mounts are often selected when dashboard space is limited.

Seat Rail Mounts

Seat rail systems attach to existing seat mounting points and provide a highly stable platform for larger devices.

These mounts are commonly used in heavy-duty commercial vehicles.

Floor Mount Systems

Floor-mounted solutions provide additional support and stability, particularly for larger tablets used as ELD interfaces.

These systems often feature adjustable arms and multiple positioning options.

Durability

Cable Management

Many ELD devices require charging or data connections.

Proper mounting solutions help organize cables and reduce workspace clutter.
