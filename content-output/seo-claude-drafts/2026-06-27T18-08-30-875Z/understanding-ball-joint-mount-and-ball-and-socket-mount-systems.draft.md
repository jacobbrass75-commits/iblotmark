## Why Ball Size Matters More Than You Think

A ball-and-socket mount works by seating a spherical ball inside a matching socket. Tighten the socket and friction locks the angle. Loosen it and you can reposition the device in seconds. The mechanism is simple, which is exactly why the ball diameter has to be right. A 17mm ball will rattle inside a 25mm socket. A 25mm ball will not seat in a 22mm socket at all. Forcing the wrong combination damages both parts.

The most common ball sizes you will encounter are:

  
- **17mm:** Used by Garmin GPS devices and a wide range of consumer navigation accessories. If you own a Garmin dash mount, the ball on the back is almost certainly 17mm. Our full breakdown of [Garmin ball mount sizing](https://iboltmounts.com/blogs/news/what-size-is-the-ball-mount-on-a-garmin-gps-and-how-do-i-find-a-compatible-mount) covers this in detail.
  
- **20mm:** A mid-range standard found on many aftermarket phone and tablet holders. Less common than 17mm or 25mm but worth identifying before you buy an arm.
  
- **22mm:** Sits between 20mm and 25mm. Several tablet holders and heavier-duty consumer mounts use this size.
  
- **25mm (1-inch, B-size):** The most widely used standard in professional and commercial mounting. RAM Mounts calls this their B-size. Most iBOLT arms, bases, and heavy-duty holders are built around the 25mm ball.
  
- **38mm (1.5-inch, C-size):** Found on larger industrial arms designed for heavy tablets, rugged computers, and commercial displays.

## The AMPS Pattern and How It Fits In

AMPS stands for Aeroquip Mounting Pattern Standard. It is a four-hole bolt pattern, not a ball size. Many professional device holders, Garmin cradles, and rugged tablet cases use an AMPS plate on the back. To mount an AMPS device on a ball arm, you need an AMPS adapter plate that accepts the four-hole pattern on one side and presents a ball socket on the other. The ball size on that adapter determines which arm it connects to. This is where buyers often get confused: they search for an **AMPS adapter** without specifying the ball size, then receive a part that does not match their arm.

Before ordering any adapter, measure or confirm three things: the ball diameter on your existing arm, the socket size on your holder or cradle, and the weight of the device you are mounting. Heavier devices need metal construction throughout the load path, not just at the base.

## Measuring Before You Order

The single most common support question we receive is from buyers who ordered the wrong adapter because they estimated the ball size by eye. Ball sizes are close enough that a 17mm and a 20mm look nearly identical without calipers. Here is a reliable process:

  
- Use digital calipers to measure the ball diameter at its widest point.
  
- Check the socket opening on the receiving part, not just the ball on the sending part.
  
- Note the device weight, because adapters have load ratings and stacking multiple adapters reduces the effective rating.
  
- Confirm arm length and reach so the adapter does not push the device out of comfortable viewing range.
  
- Check whether your surface type, dash, rail, desk, or pole, affects which base you should pair with the arm.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What ball size does Garmin use?

Most Garmin GPS devices use a 17mm ball on their cradle or mount base. If you want to connect a Garmin cradle to a 25mm arm, you need a 25mm-to-17mm adapter such as the iBOLT 25mm to Dual 17mm Metal Ball Adapter.

### Is 25mm the same as 1-inch?

Yes. The 25mm ball is also called the 1-inch or B-size ball. These terms refer to the same standard and the parts are interchangeable.

### Can I stack two adapters to go from 25mm to 17mm to a smaller size?

Technically possible, but not recommended. Each adapter junction adds play and reduces the effective load rating. Use a single adapter that bridges the two sizes you need directly.

### What is an AMPS adapter?

An AMPS adapter accepts the four-hole AMPS bolt pattern used on many device cradles and presents a ball socket on the other side. The ball size on the adapter must match your arm. Always confirm ball size when ordering an AMPS adapter.

### Do iBOLT adapters work with RAM Mounts arms?

iBOLT adapters are compatible with industry-standard ball sizes. A 25mm iBOLT adapter will seat on any arm that uses a standard 1-inch or B-size ball, regardless of brand.
