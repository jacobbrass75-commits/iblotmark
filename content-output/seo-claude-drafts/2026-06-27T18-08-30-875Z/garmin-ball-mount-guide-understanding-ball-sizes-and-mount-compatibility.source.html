Garmin GPS devices are widely used in cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats, RVs, and outdoor applications. While Garmin devices are known for navigation accuracy and reliability, many users encounter a common challenge when replacing or upgrading a mounting system: determining which garmin ball mount is compatible with their device.

Understanding ball sizes, mounting standards, and adapter options can help users select a compatible mount without unnecessary trial and error. Fortunately, many Garmin devices utilize industry-standard mounting systems that make it easier to find replacement mounts and accessories. Most Garmin GPS models that use ball mounts are compatible with standard 17mm, 22mm, or AMPS-based mounting systems depending on the specific device model.

What Is a Garmin Ball Mount?

A Garmin ball mount is a mounting system that uses a ball-and-socket connection to secure a GPS device. The ball joint allows the device to rotate, tilt, and adjust to different viewing angles while maintaining a secure connection.

Ball mounts are commonly used because they provide:

  • Flexible positioning
  • Easy installation
  • Compatibility with multiple mounting bases
  • Stable device support
  • Quick device adjustment

The ball component attaches to a mounting arm or base, while the GPS holder connects to the device itself. The size of the ball determines compatibility between components. 

Common Garmin Ball Mount Sizes

17mm Ball Mount

The 17mm ball is one of the most common mounting standards used by Garmin GPS devices.

Popular Garmin models that utilize 17mm ball mounts include:

  • Garmin Drive 52
  • Garmin dēzl OTR500
  • Garmin dēzl OTR700
  • Garmin Overlander
  • Garmin Catalyst
  • Garmin zūmo XT

Because 17mm is an industry-standard size, users can often find compatible mounting arms, suction mounts, dashboard mounts, vent mounts, and clamp mounts from various manufacturers. 

22mm Ball Mount

Certain larger Garmin navigation systems use a 22mm ball mount.

Examples include:

  • Garmin RV 1090
  • Garmin RV 890
  • Garmin dēzl OTR800
  • Garmin dēzl OTR1000

The larger ball diameter provides increased stability for heavier GPS devices and larger displays. 

AMPS Pattern Mounts

Not all Garmin devices use a ball mount directly. Some models rely on the industry-standard AMPS mounting pattern, a four-hole rectangular mounting layout spaced approximately 30mm by 38mm.

Common Garmin devices that support AMPS mounting include:

  • GPSMAP 276Cx
  • GPSMAP 66i
  • GPSMAP 66sr
  • Montana series
  • Garmin Tread series
  • Certain zūmo motorcycle GPS units

AMPS-compatible devices can often be connected to ball mount systems through adapter plates. 

Why Ball Size Matters

One of the most important factors when selecting a Garmin mounting solution is matching the correct ball size.

A 17mm socket will not properly fit a 22mm ball, and vice versa. Even small differences in diameter can affect stability and device security.

Using the wrong size may result in:

  • Loose connections
  • Excessive vibration
  • Limited adjustability
  • Increased wear
  • Potential device damage

Verifying the ball size before purchasing replacement components helps ensure proper compatibility. 

How to Identify Your Garmin Mount Type

Check the Existing Mount

The simplest method is to inspect the original mount supplied with the device. Measuring the ball diameter with a caliper or ruler can help determine compatibility.

Review Device Documentation

Garmin product manuals and mounting specifications often indicate whether a device uses a 17mm ball, 22mm ball, or AMPS mounting pattern.

Look for an AMPS Pattern

If the back of the device cradle contains four mounting holes arranged in a rectangle, it likely supports the AMPS mounting standard. This standard is widely used across vehicle, motorcycle, marine, and industrial mounting systems.

Many Garmin devices use industry-standard mounting interfaces, making them compatible with a wide range of third-party mounting systems.
