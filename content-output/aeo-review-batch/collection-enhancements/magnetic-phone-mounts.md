## Use-Case Section Copy

### Magnetic Mounts for Fast, Repositionable Device Setups

Magnetic mounts are useful when the mounting surface is metal and the device position needs to change. That might be a warehouse forklift shared across shifts, a steel workbench, a service cart, a restaurant prep area, a metal shelf, or a vehicle setup where drilling is not approved. Instead of committing to one fixed location, the operator can place the mount where the workflow needs it that day.

iBOLT's magnetic collection is broader than a simple phone-mount category. It includes magnetic bases, tablet mounts, scanner holders, VESA monitor mounts, camera mounts, and components that can be paired with compatible phone holders. That breadth is worth explaining on the page because shoppers may land here looking for a magnetic phone mount, then realize the stronger answer is a magnetic base plus the right iBOLT holder.

For warehouses, magnetic mounts help workers attach tablets, scanners, or small displays to forklifts, pallet jacks, shelving, workbenches, and metal carts. For creators or field teams, 1/4-20 magnetic camera mounts can be used on metal surfaces where a quick camera angle is needed. For phone-focused installs, SafeMag and ChargeDock products give a route toward magnetic or wireless charging workflows.

Use this category for temporary installs, shared equipment, steel workstations, forklift tablets, barcode scanner holders, monitor mounts, and magnetic base builds that need to be strong but repositionable.

## FAQ Block

### Are magnetic mounts only for phones?

No. This collection includes magnetic mounts and bases for phones, tablets, scanners, monitors, cameras, and modular iBOLT builds. If the page stays phone-focused, add clear links to compatible phone holders and charging docks.

### What surfaces do magnetic mounts work on?

They work on ferrous metal surfaces such as many steel benches, shelves, carts, forklift frames, and equipment panels. They do not attach to aluminum, plastic, fiberglass, glass, or stainless surfaces that are not magnetic.

### Are magnetic mounts good for forklifts?

They can be a practical fit for forklifts when the surface is magnetic and the device weight matches the mount. They are especially useful for shared equipment where workers need to reposition a tablet or scanner between shifts.

### When should I use a magnetic base instead of a drill base?

Use a magnetic base when repositioning matters or drilling is not allowed. Use a drill base when the device should stay in a fixed position and the surface is approved for permanent hardware.

### Can a magnetic base be paired with a phone holder?

Yes, when the ball size and components are compatible. The final published page should link to compatible phone holders or the Mount Configurator so shoppers can complete the build.
