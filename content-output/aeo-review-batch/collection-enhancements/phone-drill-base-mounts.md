## Use-Case Section Copy

### Phone Drill Base Mounts for Permanent Vehicle and Workstation Installs

Some phone mounts need to move. Others need to stay exactly where the installer put them. Phone drill-base mounts are for the second situation: fleet vehicles, work vans, equipment panels, POS counters, wall stations, motorcycle controls, and dispatch areas where a stable phone position matters more than quick removal.

iBOLT drill-base phone mounts use familiar AMPS mounting patterns and modular arms, making them easier to specify across mixed installs. A manager can standardize on drill-base hardware for company vehicles, then choose a low-profile phone holder, locking cradle, charging dock, or longer arm depending on the driver and vehicle layout.

The collection is especially useful for routes and workdays where suction mounts are not the right answer. A drill-base mount can be attached to approved dash panels, console plates, equipment surfaces, carts, counters, or wall positions. For fleets, that means fewer driver adjustments and a more consistent device location. For POS and service counters, it means the phone is always where staff expect it to be.

Use this category when the phone has a repeatable job: navigation, dispatch, proof of delivery, order entry, mobile checkout, field service, or camera capture.

## FAQ Block

### What is a phone drill-base mount?

A phone drill-base mount is a permanent or semi-permanent mount that attaches to an approved surface using screws, bolts, or an AMPS mounting plate. It is used when a phone should stay in the same position every day.

### What does AMPS mean on a phone mount?

AMPS refers to a common mounting hole pattern used across many vehicle, industrial, and electronics mounts. It helps installers pair compatible bases, arms, plates, and device holders.

### Are drill-base phone mounts better than suction mounts?

They are better for permanent installs, high-use vehicles, walls, counters, and fleet standardization. Suction mounts are better when the vehicle is leased, shared temporarily, or cannot be drilled.

### Can these mounts be used outside a vehicle?

Yes. Drill-base mounts are often useful on counters, walls, carts, equipment panels, and workstations, as long as the mounting surface and hardware are appropriate.

### Should I use a locking drill-base phone mount?

Use a locking version when the phone is company-owned, public-facing, shared between employees, or expected to remain mounted in the vehicle or workstation.
