## Use-Case Section Copy

### Locking Phone Cradles for Fleet, Delivery, and POS Setups

When a phone is part of the workflow, it needs a better home than a cup holder or a loose dash tray. Fleet phones run dispatch, maps, proof-of-delivery apps, messaging, and customer calls. Retail and restaurant phones may handle checkout, curbside pickup, loyalty apps, or mobile ordering. If that phone is shared, company-owned, or installed in a public-facing area, a basic spring cradle is often not enough.

iBOLT locking phone cradles are built for business environments where the phone needs to stay mounted, visible, and protected from casual removal. The collection includes AMPS drill-base options, multi-angle IncrediBOLT setups, and POS stands for counter use. That gives installers a path for commercial vehicles, Sprinter vans, delivery counters, wall mounts, and compact checkout stations.

The key is choosing the right level of permanence. A drill-base locking mount is the cleaner answer for a fleet phone that belongs in one van. A dual POS stand makes more sense when a phone is paired with a tablet at a checkout counter. For wall mounting, AMPS hardware gives teams a familiar, industry-standard layout that can be paired with compatible arms and plates.

Use this category when the device is valuable, assigned to a workflow, or used by multiple employees across a shift.

## FAQ Block

### What is a locking phone cradle used for?

A locking phone cradle keeps a phone secured in a specific workflow location, such as a delivery van, fleet truck, counter, wall station, or POS setup. It is useful when the phone is shared, company-issued, or customer-facing.

### Is a locking phone cradle theft-proof?

No mounted cradle should be described as theft-proof. A locking cradle is a deterrent and retention tool that helps prevent casual removal, accidental drops, and device movement during daily use.

### Which locking phone mount is best for Sprinter vans?

The Phone Dock'n Lock IncrediBOLT AMPS mount with the 4.25 inch arm is a practical fit for Sprinter vans because it gives installers more reach and angle adjustment from a fixed AMPS base.

### Can locking phone cradles be wall mounted?

Yes. AMPS drill-base locking phone mounts can be installed on approved wall surfaces, panels, carts, or counters when the substrate and hardware are appropriate for the device weight and use case.

### Do these cradles work for restaurant and retail POS?

Yes. The POS stand options are a better fit for counters, while AMPS drill-base versions are better for permanent installs where the phone needs to be held in a precise position.
