Where to Mount a Phone in a Construction Truck Cab

You pull onto a jobsite at 6:45 a.m., coffee in one hand, gate code on your phone screen. The windshield mount you installed last month is blocking the corner of your sightline, the vent clip is rattling loose from a week of rough roads, and the cup holder is occupied. Sound familiar? Choosing the right construction truck phone mount position is not a minor detail. It affects how quickly you can check a route, confirm a delivery address, or glance at an ELD reading without taking your eyes off the road longer than necessary.

Quick answer: For most construction trucks, a cup holder mount or a drilled AMPS base on the center console area gives the best combination of stability, visibility, and zero windshield obstruction. If drilling is off the table, a seat wedge mount is the next most stable no-adhesive option.

FMCSA regulations require commercial drivers to use hands-free devices and avoid reaching for phones while driving. The FMCSA mobile phone restrictions fact sheet makes clear that placement matters as much as the device itself. NHTSA also notes that reaching for a device is one of the most common distracted driving behaviors, which means a poorly placed mount creates the exact problem it is supposed to solve.

The Six Placement Positions and What Each One Costs You

Every position in a truck cab involves a trade-off. Here is a practical breakdown you can use when setting up a vehicle or standardizing a fleet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a windshield mount in a commercial truck?

Some states restrict windshield-mounted devices for commercial vehicles. Check your state's regulations and consider a console or cup holder position to avoid any compliance questions.

Will a cup holder mount stay stable on unpaved roads?

The iBOLT xProDock Bizmount Console is designed to be vibration-resistant in fleet and trucking environments. Fit matters, so confirm your cup holder diameter falls between 2.5 and 3.5 inches before ordering.

Does the seat wedge mount work in trucks with bucket seats?

Yes. The iBOLT xProDock Bizmount Wedge is designed to fit between a bucket seat and center console, which is the standard configuration in most full-size construction trucks.

Can I move an AMPS drill base mount to a different vehicle later?

The mount itself is permanent at the drill point, but the phone holder and arm detach from the AMPS base. You can install a second base in another vehicle and swap the arm and holder between them.
